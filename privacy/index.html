Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 27, 2026 | In Compliance with India DPDP Act 2026–2027, Google Play & Apple App Store Guidelines

Welcome to EduRain. Please read this Privacy Policy carefully before accessing or using our website located at https://edurain.in or the EduRain Mobile Application (collectively, the "Platform").

This Privacy Policy describes how EduRain Private Limited ("EduRain", "We", "Us", or "Our") collects, uses, processes, stores, and protects Your personal data and information when You visit, register, or use Our educational services, live classes, doubt solving, examinations, and applications.

Regulatory Notice: This Privacy Policy is published in compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 / 2026–2027 (DPDP Act) of India, Rule 3(1) of the Information Technology (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Rules, 2021, the Google Play Developer Policy, and the Apple App Store Review Guidelines (Guideline 5.1.1 - Data Collection and Storage).

1. Information We Collect

To deliver our academic coaching, live lectures, practice tests, and mentorship services, We collect the following categories of information:

1.1 Personal Identification & Profile Data

  • Full Name of Student & Parent
  • Mobile / WhatsApp Phone Number
  • Email Address
  • Date of Birth & Gender
  • Academic Grade / Class (Class 6th–12th, Dropper)
  • Target Exam (IIT-JEE, NEET, Foundation)
  • School / Institution Name & Board (CBSE/ICSE/State)
  • City, State & Residential Address
  • Profile Photograph (Voluntary)

1.2 Location Information

We collect approximate location data (derived from Your IP address, network carrier, or user-selected state/city).

  • Purpose: To connect students to the nearest regional CloudFront/CDN streaming server (Delhi/Mumbai) for low-latency live classes, schedule batch lectures according to Indian Standard Time (IST), and calculate applicable state GST for course purchases.
  • Background Location: EduRain does NOT track or access precise GPS location in the background.

1.3 Mobile App & Hardware Device Permissions

In compliance with Google Play Store & Apple App Store privacy policies, EduRain requests explicit, runtime permissions strictly when required for specific educational features:

Camera Permission

Why We Ask: Required only when You choose to scan handwritten homework/doubt questions, capture answer sheets during online practice exams, or upload a profile picture. The camera is NEVER accessed without Your explicit manual action and is never used in the background.

Microphone Permission

Why We Ask: Required only when You actively participate in interactive two-way live doubt sessions, verbal Q&A with master teachers, or voice-based doubt queries. The microphone is NEVER recorded outside of an active interactive session initiated by You.

Photos, Storage & Media Files

Why We Ask: Required strictly to allow You to select assignment photos from Your gallery, upload test solutions, and cache offline study PDF materials for review when internet bandwidth is limited.

Push Notifications

Why We Ask: Used to alert students about upcoming live classes (15 minutes prior), new study modules, test results, doubt answers, and critical academic announcements. You can toggle notifications off at any time in device settings.

1.4 Academic Usage & Performance Records

To provide analytics to students and parents, We record course progress, video lecture watch duration, mock test marks, rank percentile, attendance in live classes, and mentor doubt interaction logs.

1.5 Technical Telemetry & Crash Reports

We automatically collect device model, operating system version, browser user-agent, network type (Wi-Fi/4G/5G), and diagnostic crash logs via secure services (Sentry / Firebase Crashlytics) to fix technical bugs and optimize streaming performance on budget smartphones.

2. How We Use Your Information (Lawful Purpose)

Under the DPDP Act and consumer protection regulations, We process personal data solely for legitimate educational purposes:

  • Authenticating student logins & protecting accounts
  • Conducting interactive live classes & video streaming
  • Evaluating test series, assignments & generating report cards
  • One-on-one doubt clearing with faculty
  • Processing course enrollment fee payments securely
  • Sending SMS/WhatsApp alerts for class timings & exam schedules
  • Complying with statutory taxation, accounting, and legal rules

3. Sharing & Disclosure of Information

We never sell, rent, or trade Your personal data to third parties for advertising or commercial marketing. Data is shared strictly on a need-to-know basis with trusted infrastructure sub-processors under legally binding confidentiality agreements:

Cloud & Media Infrastructure (AWS & CloudFront)

All course assets, notes, and profile pictures are stored securely in AWS S3 (ap-south-1 Mumbai) and served via Amazon CloudFront CDN under Origin Access Control.

Secure Payment Processing (Razorpay / Certified Gateways)

Course fees are processed directly by RBI-authorized payment aggregators (PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant). EduRain never sees or stores full credit card numbers, debit card PINs, or UPI passwords.

SMS & WhatsApp Communications

Transactional alerts, OTPs, and lecture schedules are delivered via enterprise telecom aggregators strictly under TRAI DLT commercial regulations.

Legal & Law Enforcement Authorities

We may disclose information where required by court summons, law enforcement agencies, or statutory regulatory bodies under applicable Indian law.

4. Children’s Privacy & Parental Consent (< 18 Years)

EduRain caters to school-age students and exam aspirants, many of whom are below the age of 18.

  • Mandatory Parental Consent: Accounts for minor students under 18 years of age must be created with the knowledge and verified consent of a parent or legal guardian.
  • No Behavioral Advertising or Profiling: In strict alignment with the DPDP Act 2023, EduRain prohibits any behavioral tracking, profiling, or targeted commercial advertising directed toward children.
  • Safe Learning Environment: All doubt forums, classroom chat messages, and educator sessions are monitored to ensure a safe, constructive academic setting free from harassment or abusive behavior.

5. Account & Data Deletion (Apple App Store & DPDP Compliant)

Under Apple App Store Review Guideline 5.1.1(v) and the DPDP Act Right to Erasure, every student has the permanent right to delete their account and associated personal data at any time.

How to Delete Your Account and Data:

  1. In-App Self-Service (Mobile App): Open the EduRain App → Navigate to Profile → Tap "Delete Account & Data" → Confirm in the confirmation dialog.
  2. Via Email Request: Send an email from Your registered email address to support@edurain.in or info@edurain.in with the subject line "Account Deletion Request".

Data Treatment upon Deletion: Your personal profile, uploaded images, device tokens, and active sessions are permanently deleted within 30 days. Financial transaction logs are archived in encrypted cold storage strictly as required under Indian taxation and accounting laws.

6. Data Security & Storage Standards

  • Encryption: All data transmitted between Your browser/app and our servers is encrypted using 256-bit TLS 1.3 / SSL. Storage is encrypted at rest using AES-256.
  • Access Control: Backend databases and S3 storage are isolated behind VPC firewalls, AWS RDS Proxies, and Origin Access Control (OAC).
  • Security Incident Reporting: In compliance with CERT-In directions and the DPDP Act, any confirmed security vulnerability or incident will be reported to statutory authorities and affected users within required timeframes.
