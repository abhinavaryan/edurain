Frequently Asked Questions

Who is eligible to join the JEE 2027 batch?

+

The White Coat Batch is ideal for students preparing for NEET 2027, including Class 11 students and anyone looking for a structured online preparation program.

What subjects and study materials are included in this course?

+

The course covers the complete NEET syllabus in physics, chemistry, and biology, along with study materials, regular practice questions, mock tests, and revision sessions.

Will I be able to access class recordings if I miss a live session?

+

Yes, all live classes are recorded and shared with enrolled students, allowing you to revise concepts or catch up on missed lessons anytime

Do you conduct tests and doubt-clearing sessions?

+

Yes, regular chapter-wise tests, mock exams and doubt-clearing sessions are part of every course, along with detailed performance analysis to track your progress.

Fees vary by course and batch. Reach out to us by phone, email or WhatsApp and our team will share the current fee structure and any ongoing offers.
