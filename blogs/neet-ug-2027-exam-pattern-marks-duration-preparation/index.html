NEET UG 2027 Exam Pattern: Subjects, Marks, Duration, and Marking Scheme | EduRain Blog

NEET UG is the entrance test for MBBS, BDS, and AYUSH courses in India. Knowing the NEET UG 2027 Exam Pattern helps students plan their time, subject order, and accuracy. This guide covers subjects, marks, duration, the marking scheme, and what NTA has not yet confirmed. All details are based on information available till now.

What Is Confirmed and What Is Still Expected for NEET 2027

Till now, NTA has not released the NEET UG 2027 Information Bulletin. The pattern below follows the latest official NEET UG 2026 format, and any change will be confirmed only in the 2027 bulletin.

Treat the estimates as planning guides only. Check neet.nta.nic.in for official notices.

NEET UG Subjects and Question Distribution

NEET UG tests Physics, Chemistry, and Biology, where Biology covers Botany and Zoology. The latest pattern has 180 compulsory questions: 45 each in Physics, Chemistry, Botany, and Zoology, totalling 720 marks.

All questions are multiple choice with four options. The latest pattern has no optional questions and no Section B. Biology carries 360 marks, which is half of the paper.

NEET Marking Scheme and Negative Marking Rules

Each correct answer earns 4 marks, each incorrect answer loses 1 mark, and unattempted questions carry zero. This marking scheme is applied in the latest official pattern and is expected to continue unless NTA announces otherwise.

ResponseMarks
Correct answer4 marks awarded
Incorrect answer1 mark deducted
Unattempted question0 marks


Consider a student who attempts 150 questions, with 120 correct and 30 incorrect. The score is 480 minus 30, which gives 450 marks. The 30 unattempted questions add nothing.

A wrong answer also gives up the 4 marks a correct answer would have earned. A random guess among four options has an expected gain of only 0.25 marks. After removing two options, the expected gain rises to 1.5 marks per guess. Elimination matters more than luck.

NEET Exam Duration and Time Management

The latest pattern allows 3 hours, which is 180 minutes, for 180 questions. That averages one minute per question, so time planning matters. Confirm the final duration in the 2027 bulletin.

The plan below is a practice suggestion, not an official rule. Adjust it through mock tests.

SectionSuggested time
Physics55 minutes
Chemistry45 minutes
Botany and Zoology60 minutes
Final review20 minutes

Physics numericals take longer, while many Biology questions can be answered quickly. Skip a stuck question and return to it later.

Will NEET UG 2027 Be a Computer Based Test?

Reports indicate that NTA plans to shift NEET UG to computer-based test mode from 2027. This remains unconfirmed until the official bulletin states the mode, shifts, and centre rules.

The official position is simple. No 2027 bulletin exists yet, so mode, shifts, and centre rules are not final. The shift to computer-based testing is a report, not an announcement in a bulletin.

Step-by-Step Preparation Plan Based on the Pattern

Study the pattern first, then plan preparation around it: complete NCERT Biology, build Physics and Chemistry practice, take timed mock tests, and review negative marks weekly. Use the official bulletin to confirm changes.

Knowing the NEET UG 2027 Exam Pattern lets students split study time in proportion to marks. Follow these steps:

  1. Read the official bulletin when NTA releases it, and note any change in mode, marks, or duration.
  2. Finish NCERT Class 11 and 12 Biology first, since Botany and Zoology together carry 360 marks.
  3. Practise Physics numericals daily, with attention to units and calculation speed.
  4. Divide Chemistry practice among Physical, Organic, and Inorganic Chemistry.
  5. Attempt full-length mock tests of 180 questions in 180 minutes every week.
  6. Review each test and list the marks lost to negative marking.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

FAQ 1: Has NTA released the NEET UG 2027 Information Bulletin?

Till now, no. Reports expect the notification around February 2027. Rely on neet.nta.nic.in for the official bulletin and any changes in rules.

FAQ 2: How many questions and marks are there in NEET UG?

The latest pattern has 180 compulsory multiple-choice questions for 720 marks. Physics, Chemistry, Botany, and Zoology each have 45 questions worth 180 marks.

FAQ 3: What is the negative marking in NEET UG?

Each correct answer gives 4 marks, and each incorrect answer deducts 1 mark. Unattempted questions score zero. This was the scheme in the latest official pattern.

FAQ 4: How many marks does Biology carry in NEET UG?

Biology carries 360 marks from 90 questions, split equally between Botany and Zoology. That is half of the 720 total marks.

FAQ 5: How much time is available per question in NEET UG?

The latest pattern gives 180 minutes for 180 questions, which averages one minute each. Numerical Physics questions usually take longer, so quick Biology answers help balance the time.

FAQ 6: Does the latest NEET UG pattern have Section B or optional questions?

No. The latest pattern has 180 compulsory questions with no Section B and no choice between questions. Confirm this again in the 2027 bulletin.
