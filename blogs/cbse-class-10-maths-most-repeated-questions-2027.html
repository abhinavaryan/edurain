CBSE Class 10 Maths Most Repeated Questions in Board Exams | EduRain Blog

CBSE Class 10 Maths Most Repeated Questions come mainly from Algebra, Geometry, Trigonometry, and Statistics. Past papers often feature quadratic equations, arithmetic progressions, similar triangles, tangents to circles, trigonometric identities, heights and distances, and probability. This is a pattern from earlier papers, not an official list, so every unit still needs revision.

CBSE will hold the Class 10 board exams from 16 February to 9 March 2027. Check the official date sheet for your Maths paper date. This guide gives you the marks pattern, paper structure, chapter-wise question types, formulas, common mistakes, and practice questions.

Which chapters are important in Class 10 Maths 2027?

Algebra carries the highest weightage at 20 marks, followed by Geometry with 15 and Trigonometry with 12. CBSE sets the 80-mark theory paper across seven units, so every unit needs steady revision.

The theory paper carries 80 marks, and 20 marks go to internal assessment, making the total 100. The syllabus has seven units and 14 NCERT chapters. CBSE gives marks by unit, not by chapter.

How is the Class 10 Maths board paper structured?

The paper has 38 compulsory questions in five sections, A to E, for 80 marks. Section A has 20 one-mark questions, and Section E has three case-based questions of four marks each.

Repeated questions usually test core skills. These include HCF and LCM, nature of roots, nth term of an AP, similarity and tangent properties, trigonometric identities, heights and distances, sector area, mean or median, and probability.

How to prepare for CBSE Class 10 Maths Board Exam?

Finish NCERT examples and exercises first, revise formulas daily, and solve sample papers in timed sessions. Review every error. Give extra time to Algebra, Geometry, and Trigonometry, because together they carry 47 marks.

Practice matters more than reading in Maths. Whatever you choose, make sure the classes explain methods clearly instead of only giving answers.

  1. Complete NCERT examples and exercises for all 14 chapters.
  2. Write a one-page formula sheet and revise it every morning.
  3. Solve Class 10 Maths previous year questions with detailed solutions, then check each step of your own working.
  4. Attempt CBSE sample papers in three hours, the length of the board paper.
  5. Keep a mistake log and redo every wrong question after two days.

Which formulas should students revise before the board exam?

Revise the discriminant, AP formulas, distance and section formulas, trigonometric identities, and sector area. These formulas support many repeated question types. Write each one with its chapter name so recall becomes quick during the exam.

ChapterFormula
Real NumbersHCF × LCM = product of the two numbers
PolynomialsSum of zeros = −b/a; product of zeros = c/a
Pair of Linear EquationsUnique solution if a₁/a₂ ≠ b₁/b₂; no solution if a₁/a₂ = b₁/b₂ ≠ c₁/c₂; infinite solutions if all three ratios are equal

Arithmetic Progressionsaₙ = a + (n − 1)d; Sₙ = n/2 [2a + (n − 1)d]

TrianglesIf DE ∥ BC, then AD/DB = AE/EC

What common mistakes cost marks in Class 10 Maths?

Students lose marks by skipping steps, keeping invalid answers, misreading class intervals, and using the wrong value of π. Writing each step with a reason protects marks even when a final calculation goes wrong.

MistakeHow to avoid it
Incomplete proofsWrite the given data, what to prove, any construction, and a reason for each step
Keeping invalid answersReject negative values for length, time, or age in word problems
Wrong value of πUse the value given in the question; use 22/7 only when the question allows it
Class interval errorsConvert inclusive classes to continuous classes before finding the median or mode
Poor figures in trigonometryDraw the angle of elevation or depression from the horizontal line
Sign errors in formulasWrite each substitution in brackets before simplifying

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

FAQ 1: Which unit has the highest weightage in Class 10 Maths?

Algebra has the highest weightage with 20 of the 80 theory marks. It includes Polynomials, Pair of Linear Equations in Two Variables, Quadratic Equations, and Arithmetic Progressions.

FAQ 2: Are repeated questions guaranteed to come in the board exam?

No. Repeated question types show a pattern from earlier papers, but CBSE does not publish or confirm them. Prepare the full syllabus and use the pattern only to prioritise revision.

FAQ 3: Which formulas are most useful for Class 10 Maths?

The discriminant, AP formulas, distance and section formulas, trigonometric identities, and sector area formulas are very useful. They support several question types across Algebra, Coordinate Geometry, Trigonometry, and Mensuration.

FAQ 4: What is the paper structure of Class 10 Maths?

The 80-mark paper has Section A with 20 questions, Section B with 5, Section C with 6, Section D with 4, and Section E with 3 case-based questions. Section A carries one mark each, and the other sections carry two, three, five, and four marks.

FAQ 5: How many marks are needed to pass Class 10 Maths?

Students need at least 33% marks in each subject to be declared a pass. Confirm the exact passing rules on the official CBSE website.

FAQ 6: Is the Basic Maths paper different from the Standard paper?

Both papers follow the same seven units and five sections. The Standard paper gives more marks to higher-order thinking, while the Basic paper gives more marks to recall and understanding. Prepare according to the paper you have chosen.
