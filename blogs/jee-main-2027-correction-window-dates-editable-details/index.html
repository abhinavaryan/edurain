JEE Main 2027 Correction Window: Dates, Editable Details, Process & Important Guidelines | EduRain Blog

JEE Main 2027 Correction Window: Dates, Editable Details – The JEE Main 2027 Session 1 correction window will remain open from 27 November to 29 November 2026, until 11:50 PM, on the official website, jeemain.nta.nic.in. Only candidates who have successfully submitted their application forms can use this facility, and changes are allowed only in the fields enabled by NTA. This guide covers important dates, editable details, correction fees, the step-by-step process, and key guidelines candidates must follow.

What Are the Official JEE Main 2027 Application Correction Dates?

This section brings together the JEE Main 2027 Correction Window: Dates and Editable Details for Session 1 so you can plan your edits before the deadline. The facility is online only, and it is short- just three days.

EventSession 1Session 2 (reported)
Correction window opens27 November 20268 February 2027
Last date for correction29 November 2026, 11:50 PM10 February 2027, 11:50 PM
Exam dates20, 21, 22, 23, 24 and 28 January 2027April 2027: verify the official notice

Session 1 registration closed on 22 November 2026. Late applications were accepted from 23 to 26 November with a ₹500 late fee. The correction window followed immediately after.

Because the window lasts only three days, log in on the first day. An early start leaves time to retry if an upload or payment fails.

Which Details Can Students Modify in JEE Main 2027 Registration?

Candidates can generally edit name fields, parent names, Class 10 and 12 details, date of birth, gender, category, exam city, medium, paper, and signature. NTA enables only specific fields, so the portal list on the correction day is final.

Use JEE Main 2027 Correction Window: Dates, Editable Details as a planning checklist, and remember that the live portal has the final say. Education portals report the fields below.

Match your name, date of birth, and category with your Class 10 and Class 12 records. Reports say name corrections are limited, so check which name field is open before editing. Mismatches can create trouble during later document verification.

Is There Any Fee for JEE Main Form Correction 2027?

Most corrections reportedly carry no extra charge. Category changes, adding an exam paper, or choosing a foreign exam city may need an additional fee. The portal shows the exact amount before you pay.

A category change may require the difference in the application fee for the revised category. Adding a paper may require the fee for that paper. Keep the payment receipt and the updated confirmation page safe.

How to Edit the JEE Main 2027 Application Form Step by Step?

Visit jeemain.nta.nic.in, open the correction link, log in with your application number and password, edit the enabled fields, pay any extra fee, submit, and download the updated confirmation page. Complete everything before 11:50 PM on 29 November 2026.

Before logging in, keep your application number, password, Class 10 and Class 12 documents, and any category or PwD certificate ready. If you plan to change your signature, keep a fresh scan as per portal instructions. Use a reliable online payment method in case a fee appears.

  1. Open jeemain.nta.nic.in and click the Session 1 application correction link.
  2. Enter your application number and password to log in.
  3. Open the submitted form and review every field.
  4. Edit only the incorrect details in the enabled fields.
  5. Upload a corrected signature if you are changing it.
  6. Pay the additional fee if the portal asks for it.
  7. Preview the revised form and submit it.
  8. Download the updated confirmation page and save it.

After saving the confirmation page, shift your attention to revision, because Session 1 starts on 20 January 2027. What Common Mistakes Should Students Avoid During Correction?

Typical mistakes include waiting for the last day, editing without checking locked fields, ignoring fee prompts, and skipping the final confirmation page. Check each detail against your Class 10 and 12 documents before submitting.

  1. Waiting for the last evening, when heavy traffic can slow the process.
  2. Changing category without keeping the relevant certificate ready.
  3. Choosing an exam city without reading the address rules in the information bulletin.
  4. Paying a fee but not downloading the updated confirmation page.
  5. Trusting unofficial lists instead of the fields shown on the portal.

Category and paper changes need extra care because they can change the fee. Read every prompt on the payment screen before you confirm.

The Session 1 dates and the 11:50 PM deadline are consistent across reports citing NTA notices. Field lists, fee amounts, and the number of allowed edits come from education portals, so verify them on jeemain.nta.nic.in or in the bulletin.

Key Takeaways

  1. The Session 1 correction window runs from 27 to 29 November 2026 and closes at 11:50 PM.
  2. Only fields enabled on the portal can be edited, and mobile number, email, address, and photograph are reported as locked.
  3. Category change, an added exam paper, or a foreign exam city may add a fee.
  4. Log in with your application number and password, then download the updated confirmation page.
  5. Verify every field list and fee on jeemain.nta.nic.in, since reports can differ.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

FAQ 1: When will the JEE Main 2027 correction window open?

It opens on 27 November 2026 for Session 1 and closes on 29 November 2026 at 11:50 PM. The Session 2 window is reported for 8 to 10 February 2027.

FAQ 2: Which details can be corrected in JEE Main 2027?

Reported editable fields include name fields, Class 10 and 12 details, date of birth, gender, category, exam city, medium, paper, and signature. Mobile number, email, address, and photograph are reported as locked.

FAQ 3: Can I change my category in the JEE Main 2027 application form?

Category correction is reported as permitted, and it may attract an additional fee based on the revised category. Keep the relevant certificate ready and follow the bulletin rules.

FAQ 4: Can I change my exam centre in JEE Main 2027?

An exam city change is permitted according to the applicable address rules. A foreign exam city may need an extra fee. Check the available options on the portal after logging in.

FAQ 5: Is there any fee for JEE Main form correction 2027?

Most corrections reportedly carry no extra fee. Category change, adding an exam paper, and a foreign exam city may need extra payment, and the portal displays the exact amount.

FAQ 6: How many times can I edit the JEE Main application form?

Education portals describe the correction window as a one-time facility, so check every field before you submit. Confirm the rule in the NTA bulletin on jeemain.nta.nic.in.
