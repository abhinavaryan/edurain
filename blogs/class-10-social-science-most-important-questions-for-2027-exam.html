Class 10 Social Science Most Important Questions for Board Exam 2027 | EduRain Blog

The most important questions for the Class 10 Social Science board exam 2027 come from four units. These are History, Geography, Political Science, and Economics. Each unit carries 20 marks in the 80-mark theory paper. This guide lists the Class 10 Social Science Most Important Questions by unit, with short answers, map tips, and a preparation plan.

What Is the CBSE Class 10 SST Paper Pattern for 2027?

The theory paper carries 80 marks, and internal assessment carries 20 marks. History, Geography, Political Science, and Economics get 20 marks each. Map pointing adds 5 marks, included within History and Geography.

Question typeMarks eachNumber of questionsTotal marks
Multiple choice12020
Very short answer248
Short answer3515
Case based4312
Long answer5420
Map pointing515

Map marks are split as 2 for History and 3 for Geography. The question type split above follows the pattern published in recent sample papers. Confirm it against the official 2026 to 2027 sample paper on cbseacademic. nic.in before you finalise your plan.

How to Prepare for Class 10 Social Science Board Exam 2027?

Start with the official syllabus, finish NCERT line by line, then practise written answers and maps every week. CBSE released the 2027 date sheet on November 6, 2026. Class 10 exams begin on February 16, 2027, and end on March 9, 2027.

  1. Download the syllabus and mark every chapter as done, weak, or strong.
  2. Read each NCERT chapter fully, including boxes, case studies, and in-text questions.
  3. Write short notes with dates, names, terms, and definitions.
  4. Practise 3-mark and 5-mark answers in timed sessions.
  5. Draw and label the listed maps once every week.
  6. Solve CBSE sample papers and previous-year papers under exam conditions.
  7. Revise weak chapters twice before the exam.

Which Chapter-Wise Questions Should Students Practise First?

Start with questions that test core NCERT ideas in each unit. The table below gives a practice set of Class 10 Social Science Most Important Questions with the points a good answer should include.

UnitQuestionKey points for the answer
HistoryWhy did Gandhiji withdraw the Non-Cooperation Movement?Violence at Chauri Chaura in February 1922, where a police station was set on fire. Gandhiji felt satyagrahis needed proper training in nonviolence.
HistoryWhat was the role of Giuseppe Mazzini in Italian unification?Founded Young Italy and Young Europe. Wanted a unitary republic. Inspired young nationalists.
HistoryWhy was the Silk Route important for world trade?Linked Asia with Europe and Africa. Carried silk, textiles, and spices. Spread ideas, religions, and culture.
GeographyWhat is soil erosion? Name two methods of soil conservation.Removal of soil cover by wind and water. Contour ploughing, terrace farming, or shelterbelts.
GeographyWhy is the iron and steel industry called a basic industry?It supplies raw material to machine tools, construction, and other industries.
GeographyWhat are multipurpose river projects? Give one benefit and one concern.Dams that serve irrigation, power, and flood control. Benefit: electricity. Concern: displacement of people or loss of habitat.
Political ScienceExplain the Belgian model of power sharing.Equal numbers of Dutch- and French-speaking ministers in the central government. A separate community government. Brussels has equal representation.
Political ScienceHow does federalism differ from a unitary system?Federalism has two or more levels of government with their own powers. A unitary system has one level or subordinate units.
Political ScienceState two functions of political parties.Contest elections, form governments, act as opposition, and make policies.
EconomicsDifferentiate between organised and unorganised sectors.Organised: registered, fixed hours, job security. Unorganised: scattered units, irregular work, low pay.
EconomicsHow does the RBI supervise formal sources of credit?Banks keep a cash balance, submit information, and must lend to small farmers and small borrowers.
EconomicsWhat is globalisation? Name one factor that promoted it.Rapid integration of countries through trade and investment. Technology or trade liberalisation.

You can find more CBSE Class 10 social science important questions with answers 2027 in NCERT exercises and CBSE sample papers. Cover important history, geography, civics, and economics questions for Class 10 in equal measure.

For map work, practise the items listed in the official syllabus. Geography maps usually involve locating items such as major ports and dams.

What Common Mistakes Lower Marks in Social Science?

Most lost marks come from weak maps, long answers without keywords, and careless reading of source passages. Each mistake below has a simple fix.

MistakeFix
Skipping map practicePractise listed maps weekly. Map marks are fixed and scored.
Writing long paragraphs for 3-mark answersWrite three clear points with keywords.
Ignoring the passage in case-based questionsRead the source twice and quote its details.
Mixing up dates, names and placesRevise a one-page timeline for each history chapter.
Memorising without understandingExplain each concept aloud in your own words.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

FAQ 1: What are the most important questions for Class 10 Social Science board exam 2027?

They come from NCERT concepts in History, Geography, Political Science, and Economics. Focus on causes and effects, definitions, differences, map work, and case-based passages. The practice table above gives a starting set.

FAQ 2: Which chapters are important in Class 10 SST?

All prescribed chapters can appear, so none should be skipped. Geography needs the most revision time because it has seven chapters. History needs steady answer-writing practice.

FAQ 3: How to prepare for Class 10 Social Science board exam 2027?

Study the official syllabus, finish NCERT, write short notes, and practise maps weekly. Solve sample papers and previous year papers in timed sessions. Revise weak chapters twice before the exam.

FAQ 4: What are the most repeated questions in Class 10 Social Science?

CBSE does not release an official list. Themes such as nationalism, resources, power sharing, sectors of the economy, and credit appear often. Use them as revision priorities, not as guaranteed questions.

FAQ 5: Where can I find chapter-wise SST important questions for Class 10?

FAQ 6: How many marks is the Class 10 Social Science board exam?

The theory paper is 80 marks. Internal assessment adds 20 marks, so the subject totals 100 marks. Each of the four units carries 20 marks in theory.
