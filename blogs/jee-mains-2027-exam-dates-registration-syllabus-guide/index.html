JEE Mains 2027: Exam Dates, Registration, Syllabus, Pattern & Latest Updates | EduRain Blog

JEE Mains 2027: Exam Dates, Registration, Syllabus details are now partly announced by NTA. Session 1 registration opened on 24 October 2026 and, as per reports, closes on 22 November 2026. Paper 1 is a three-hour computer-based test of 75 questions for 300 marks. Always confirm every detail on jeemain.nta.nic.in. This blog covers dates, the application form, syllabus, pattern, eligibility, and preparation for JEE Main 2027.

When Are the JEE Main 2027 Exam Dates?

As per reports on the NTA schedule, JEE Main Session 1 2027 is planned for 20 to 24 January and 28 January 2027. Session 2 is planned for 2 to 7 April 2027. Confirm final dates on the NTA website.

The Session 1 date list was revised after an earlier schedule, so always check the latest notice. Entries marked expected below come from trend-based reports. Only the NTA bulletin is final.

How Does JEE Main 2027 Registration Work?

Candidates apply online at jeemain.nta.nic.in by creating an account, filling the JEE Main application form 2027, uploading documents, and paying the fee. Session 1 registration opened on 24 October 2026.

Follow these steps for JEE Main 2027 registration:

  1. Register with a valid mobile number and email address.
  2. Fill in personal and academic details and select the paper.
  3. Upload the photograph, signature, and category documents in the specified format.
  4. Pay the fee online through net banking, UPI, or card.
  5. Download and save the confirmation page.

Reported Session 1 fees range from Rs 500 to Rs 1,000 depending on category and gender. A late fee of Rs 500 is reported on top of this. Check the bulletin for the exact amounts.

What Is the JEE Main 2027 Syllabus?

The JEE Main 2027 syllabus covers Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics based on the Class 11 and Class 12 curriculum. NTA publishes the official topic list on its website, so students should download it directly.

The table lists broad areas only. Use the NTA document for the final chapter list.

SubjectBroad areas
PhysicsMechanics, thermodynamics, electrostatics, current electricity, magnetism, optics, modern physics
ChemistryPhysical chemistry, organic chemistry, inorganic chemistry
MathematicsAlgebra, trigonometry, calculus, coordinate geometry, vectors, probability

Class 11 and 12 together contain a large number of chapters. A structured plan helps students finish them before Session 1. Some students pick the Best JEE Main 2027 Online Coaching programme for chapter-wise schedules and weekly tests, while others study alone with NCERT books and previous papers. Whichever route you choose, check that the plan covers every topic in the NTA list.

What Is the JEE Main Exam Pattern 2027?

Paper 1 for BE and BTech is a computer-based test of three hours with 75 questions and 300 marks. Each subject has 20 multiple-choice and 5 numerical value questions.

ModeComputer-based test

Marking+4 for correct, minus 1 for incorrect

Paper 2A (BArch) is reported to include mathematics, an aptitude test, and a drawing test. Paper 2B (BPlanning) is reported to include mathematics, an aptitude test, and planning-based questions. Both are reported to be 3 hours 30 minutes and 400 marks. This JEE Main exam pattern 2027 is based on published reports, so verify it in the bulletin.

Who Meets the JEE Main Eligibility Criteria 2027?

As reported, there is no age limit for JEE Main. Candidates need to have passed Class 12 in 2025 or 2026, or be appearing in 2027. Check the NTA bulletin for accepted qualifications.

Eligibility to appear in the exam is different from eligibility for admission to a particular institute. The bulletin explains accepted qualifications. Read the admission rules of each institute separately.

How Can Students Prepare Before Session 1?

Start with the NTA syllabus, finish NCERT first, then practise chapter-wise questions and full mock tests in computer-based format. Review mistakes every week and revise formulas before each mock.

  1. Download the syllabus and mark each chapter as weak, average, or strong.
  2. Read NCERT Chemistry line by line, including tables and exceptions.
  3. Solve chapter-wise questions after finishing each topic.
  4. Attempt full-length mock tests on a computer from the first month.
  5. Keep an error notebook and revisit it weekly.
  6. Use JEE Main Session 1 2027 as a real test, and plan improvements for Session 2 2027. Check the bulletin on how scores from both sessions are used.

When reviewing the best online JEE coaching 2027 options, compare teacher quality, doubt support, and test analysis. JEE online coaching classes can add structure, but daily self-practice matters more.

Avoid these mistakes during preparation:

  1. Trusting unofficial dates without checking NTA notices.
  2. Skipping NCERT and relying only on reference books.
  3. Ignoring numerical value questions in practice.
  4. Guessing heavily despite negative marking.

Which JEE Main 2027 Updates Are Official?

Official updates appear only on jeemain.nta.nic.in in the information bulletin and public notices. Coaching sites and news portals often add expected dates, so treat them as estimates until NTA confirms them.

Even for JEE Mains 2027: Exam Dates, Registration, Syllabus, published reports disagree on points such as shift timings and the Session 1 date list, which was revised. Check the NTA website before each important step. This is especially true for fee payment and form correction deadlines.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

FAQ 1: When is JEE Main Session 1 2027?

As per reports, Session 1 is planned for 20 to 24 January and 28 January 2027. The schedule was revised once, so confirm the final dates on jeemain.nta.nic.in.

FAQ 2: When does Session 2 registration start?

Session 2 registration is expected to start on 5 January 2027 and close on 3 February 2027. These dates come from reports, so verify them in the NTA notice.

FAQ 3: What is the marking scheme for Paper 1?

Reports state that each correct answer earns 4 marks and each incorrect answer costs 1 mark. This applies to both multiple-choice and numerical value questions. Confirm this in the bulletin.

FAQ 4: Is there an age limit for JEE Main 2027?

Reports state that there is no age limit. Candidates must have passed Class 12 in 2025 or 2026, or be appearing in 2027. Read the bulletin for accepted qualifications.

FAQ 5: Which subjects and classes does the syllabus cover?

The syllabus covers Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics from the Class 11 and Class 12 curriculum. Download the official topic list from the NTA website for the final chapter details.

FAQ 6: Can students appear in both sessions?

NTA has offered two sessions in recent years, and students could appear in one or both. The bulletin explains how scores are considered, so read that section before choosing.
