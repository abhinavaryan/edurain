What Are the Key Theorems in This Chapter?


The chapter is built around a few core theorems. Every student must know these before attempting important questions.

1. Basic Proportionality Theorem (BPT)

Statement: If a line is drawn parallel to one side of a triangle, it divides the other two sides in the same ratio.

Example: In triangle ABC, if DE is parallel to BC, then:

AD/DB = AE/EC

This theorem forms the foundation of most questions in Class 10 Maths Triangles.

2. Criteria for Similarity of Triangles

Two triangles are said to be similar if their corresponding angles are equal and corresponding sides are proportional.

The three criteria are:

  1. AA (Angle-Angle): Two angles of one triangle are equal to two angles of another
  2. SAS (Side-Angle-Side): Two sides in proportion and the included angle is equal
  3. SSS (Side-Side-Side): All three sides are in the same ratio

3. Pythagoras' Theorem

Statement: In a right-angled triangle, the square of the hypotenuse equals the sum of the squares of the other two sides.

Formula: AC² = AB² + BC²

The converse is also important: If AC² = AB² + BC², then angle B is 90 degrees.

What Are the Most Important Questions from This Chapter?


Here are five high-frequency questions that appear in CBSE board exams, with solutions explained clearly.

Question 1: Prove the Basic Proportionality Theorem

Question: Prove that if a line is drawn parallel to one side of a triangle, it divides the other two sides proportionally.

Solution:

Given: Triangle ABC with DE parallel to BC

To prove: AD/DB = AE/EC

Proof (Step by Step):

  1. Draw DM perpendicular to AE and EN perpendicular to AD
  2. Area of triangle ADE = (1/2) × AD × EN
  3. Area of triangle BDE = (1/2) × DB × EN
  4. Therefore, Area(ADE) / Area(BDE) = AD/DB
  5. Similarly, Area(ADE) / Area(CDE) = AE/EC
  6. Since triangles BDE and CDE are on the same base DE and between the same parallel lines, their areas are equal
  7. Therefore, AD/DB = AE/EC — Proved

Question 2: Find the Value of x using BPT


Question: In triangle PQR, ST is parallel to QR. If PS = 3 cm, SQ = 5 cm, and PT = 6 cm, find TR.

Solution:

By BPT: PS/SQ = PT/TR

3/5 = 6/TR

TR = (6 × 5) / 3 = 10 cm

Answer: TR = 10 cm

Question 3: Prove Two Triangles Are Similar


Question: In triangles ABC and DEF, angle A = angle D = 60°, angle B = angle E = 70°. Are the triangles similar? Give a reason.

Solution:

In triangle ABC: angle A = 60°, angle B = 70°, so angle C = 50°

In triangle DEF: angle D = 60°, angle E = 70°, so angle F = 50°

All three corresponding angles are equal.

By AA similarity criterion, triangle ABC is similar to triangle DEF.

Question 4: Area of Similar Triangles


Question: The areas of two similar triangles are 64 cm² and 121 cm². If one side of the first triangle is 8 cm, find the corresponding side of the second triangle.

Solution:

For similar triangles: Area1/Area2 = (Side1)²/(Side2)²

64/121 = 64/(Side2)²

(Side2)² = (64 × 121)/64 = 121

Side2 = 11 cm

Answer: The corresponding side is 11 cm.

Question 5: Pythagoras Theorem Application


Question: A ladder 13 m long is placed against a wall. Its foot is 5 m from the wall. Find the height at which it touches the wall.

Solution:

By Pythagoras Theorem:

Hypotenuse² = Base² + Height²

13² = 5² + Height²

169 = 25 + Height²

Height² = 144

Height = 12 m

Answer: The ladder touches the wall at a height of 12 m.

How Should Students Prepare This Chapter for Board Exams?


Preparation matters as much as understanding. Here are practical tips for scoring well in this chapter:

  1. Learn the theorems with diagrams. Always draw the triangle before writing the proof.
  2. Memorize the similarity criteria. Know when to apply AA, SAS, or SSS.
  3. Practice at least 10 numerical problems from previous year CBSE papers.
  4. Write each step clearly. CBSE awards marks for each step in proofs.
  5. Revise the converse of the Pythagoras Theorem separately as it is often tested.
  6. Check the ratio carefully in BPT-based questions to avoid errors.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)


Q1. How many theorems are in Class 10 Maths Chapter 6?

There are six theorems in Chapter 6, including BPT and its converse, criteria for similarity, the theorem on areas of similar triangles, and Pythagoras' Theorem along with its converse.

Q2. Is Chapter 6 difficult for Class 10 students?

It is moderately challenging. The numerical problems are straightforward once you understand the theorems. Proofs require regular practice but become manageable with consistent effort.

Q3. How many marks does the Triangles chapter carry in CBSE boards?

This chapter generally contributes around 6 to 8 marks in the CBSE Class 10 board exam, covering MCQs, short answers, and long answer proofs.

Q4. What is the difference between congruent and similar triangles?

Congruent triangles are identical in size and angles. Similar triangles have the same angles and proportional sides, but they may differ in size.

Q5. Can I skip the proofs and only study numericals?

It is not advisable. CBSE board papers regularly include 5 mark proof-based questions from this chapter. Skipping proofs can cost you significant marks.

Q6. How much time should I spend on Chapter 6?

Most students need 5 to 7 days of focused study to cover all theorems, practice important questions, and revise properly. Daily revision of formulas and diagrams is recommended during the final weeks before the exam.
