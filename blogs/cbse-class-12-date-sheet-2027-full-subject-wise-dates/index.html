CBSE Class 12 Date Sheet 2027: Check Subject-Wise Dates Timetable & Download PDF | EduRain Blog

CBSE released the CBSE Class 12 Date Sheet 2027 on 6 November 2026 on cbse.gov.in. Theory exams run from 16 February to 9 April 2027, starting with Physics. Most papers begin at 10:30 AM. This guide lists subject-wise dates, timings, download steps, and practical planning tips for Science, Commerce, and Humanities students.

When was the CBSE 12th date sheet for 2027 released?

The board announced the Class 12 exam dates on 6 November 2026. Theory papers start on 16 February 2027 and end on 9 April 2027. Students can download the official PDF from cbse.gov.in.

EventDate
Date sheet release6 November 2026
Practical exams for winter-bound schools9 November to 19 December 2026
Practical exams for other affiliated schools1 January to 13 February 2027
Theory exams begin16 February 2027
Theory exams end9 April 2027

Schools fix the exact practical dates, so confirm them with your school. The CBSE 12th board exam date for theory papers in 2027 is already official.

Admit cards are a different matter. In 2026, the Class 12 admit card came out on 3 February for exams starting 17 February. A similar early February release is an expectation, not an official announcement.

How can PCM and PCB students plan revision around these dates?

Gaps between papers decide your revision plan. Physics and Chemistry are 11 days apart, Chemistry and English are 6 days apart, and Mathematics and Biology are 16 days apart. Plan chapter-wise revision using these gaps.

For Class 12 date sheet 2027 CBSE board PCM students, the order is Physics on 16 February, Chemistry on 27 February, English on 5 March, and Mathematics on 9 March. The gap between English and Mathematics is only four days. Keep Mathematics practice running daily from February.

PCB students have a longer stretch at the end. English falls on 5 March and Biology on 25 March, which leaves 20 days. Many students also want a clear weekly structure for revision. Edurain Online Education Platform is an educational guidance platform that students can explore for organised study support while they build a plan around these dates. Whichever resource you choose, keep NCERT textbooks and CBSE sample papers as your core material.

Commerce and Humanities students can plan like this:

  1. Commerce: Accountancy on 23 February, Economics on 16 March, Business Studies on 1 April.
  2. Humanities: Geography on 25 February, Psychology on 2 March, Political Science on 19 March, History on 27 March.

How can students download the CBSE 12th time table PDF?

Open cbse.gov.in, find the Class 12 date sheet link in the latest updates, and download the PDF. Save it and print one copy. This is the simplest route to the CBSE 12th time table 2027 PDF download.

  1. Visit the official website cbse.gov.in.
  2. Open the Class 12 date sheet link in the latest updates section.
  3. Download the PDF and save it on your phone or computer.
  4. Check each subject name, code, date, and time.
  5. Print a copy and compare it with your school's notice.

Use only the official site for the CBSE date sheet 2027 Class 12 PDF download. Screenshots shared on social media can contain errors.

What exam timings and rules should students remember?

Papers begin at 10:30 AM. Three-hour papers end at 1:30 PM, while two-hour skill and elective papers end at 12:30 PM. Reach the centre 30 to 45 minutes before the reporting time.

  1. Carry your admit card. Entry is not allowed without it.
  2. Expect an extra 15 minutes to read the question paper.
  3. Follow the instructions printed on the date sheet.
  4. Sports participants get a separate special date sheet, with papers listed for April 2027.

Class 10 students searching for the CBSE date sheet 2027, class 10 subject-wise schedule should know that the same release covers them. The first phase of Class 10 exams runs from 16 February to 9 March 2027. The second phase for Class 10 is tentatively planned for May 2027, so wait for official confirmation.

Which mistakes should students avoid while using the date sheet?

Common mistakes include trusting unofficial lists, ignoring subject codes, and giving every paper equal time. Use the official PDF and divide your time by paper difficulty and the gap between papers.

  1. Trusting unofficial lists. Verify every date on cbse.gov.in.
  2. Ignoring short gaps. English and Mathematics are only four days apart, so start Mathematics practice early.
  3. Waiting for later papers. Biology, History, and Business Studies come in the last weeks. Do not postpone their first revision.
  4. Skipping practical dates. Ask your school for the exact practical schedule.
  5. Missing the subject code. Confirm the code of each optional subject against the PDF.

Key Takeaways

  1. The official date sheet was released on 6 November 2026.
  2. Theory exams run from 16 February to 9 April 2027.
  3. Physics is first on 16 February, and Business Studies is on 1 April.
  4. Most papers run from 10:30 AM to 1:30 PM, while shorter papers end at 12:30 PM.
  5. Practical dates are fixed by schools within the board's windows.
  6. Admit card timing is an expectation based on last year, not an official date.

Conclusion and Next Steps

The 2027 schedule gives students nearly three months of clear dates. Download the PDF from cbse.gov.in, mark every paper on a wall calendar, and count the gap days between subjects. Then attempt one CBSE sample paper per subject within the exam time limit.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

FAQ 1: When do the CBSE Class 12 board exams 2027 start and end?

Theory exams start on 16 February 2027 with Physics. They end on 9 April 2027, which is the last date for papers such as Retail and Web Application.

FAQ 2: What is the date and time of the Physics exam?

Physics is on 16 February 2027. The paper runs from 10:30 AM to 1:30 PM.

FAQ 3: What are the dates for Chemistry, Mathematics, and Biology?

Chemistry is on 27 February 2027, Mathematics on 9 March 2027, and Biology on 25 March 2027. Each paper begins at 10:30 AM.

FAQ 4: Are practical exams part of the same theory schedule?

No. Schools conduct practicals within board windows. Winter-bound schools hold them from 9 November to 19 December 2026. Other schools hold them from 1 January to 13 February 2027.

FAQ 5: Where can students download the official date sheet PDF?

The official PDF is available on cbse.gov.in. Download it from the latest updates section and avoid unofficial copies.

FAQ 6: Which Commerce and Humanities papers fall in March and April?

Economics is on 16 March and Political Science on 19 March. History is on 27 March, Sociology on 30 March, and Business Studies on 1 April.
