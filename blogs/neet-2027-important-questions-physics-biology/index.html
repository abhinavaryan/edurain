NEET 2027 Important Questions: Physics & Biology Guide

Preparing for NEET 2027 requires a focused strategy built around the right study material and consistent practice. Whether you are just starting your preparation journey or already deep into revision, knowing which questions matter most can save you valuable time and effort. This article is designed for students who want to understand the most frequently tested concepts in Physics and Biology for the NEET exam. Here, you will learn about NEET 2027 Important Questions, the best ways to practice them, the role of online education platforms in your preparation, and how to build a solid study plan that covers every high-weightage topic.


What Are NEET 2027 Important Questions?


NEET 2027 Important Questions are carefully selected problems based on chapters and concepts that appear repeatedly in the National Eligibility cum Entrance Test. These questions are drawn from the NTA-prescribed syllabus, which follows NCERT textbooks for Class 11 and Class 12 Physics and Biology.


Important questions typically come from chapters with the highest weightage. Practicing them helps students:


Focus on topics that carry maximum marks

Understand the exam pattern and question style

Build speed and accuracy for the actual test

Identify weak areas early in the preparation cycle


The NTA designs the NEET paper to test conceptual clarity, application skills, and time management. Practicing high-yield questions is one of the most effective strategies to score well.


Which Physics Chapters Carry the Highest Weightage?


Physics is often considered the most challenging section in NEET. However, focusing on the right chapters can make a significant difference in your score.


Top Physics Chapters for NEET 2027


Mechanics: Laws of Motion, Work, Energy, Power, Rotational Motion

Electrodynamics: Current Electricity, Electrostatics, Electromagnetic Induction

Optics: Ray Optics, Wave Optics

Modern Physics: Dual Nature of Radiation, Atoms and Nuclei

Thermodynamics: Kinetic Theory, Heat Transfer

Waves: Sound, Standing Waves, Doppler Effect


Commonly Asked Question Types


Numerical problems from Mechanics and Electrodynamics

Conceptual questions from Optics and Modern Physics

Formula-based questions from Thermodynamics

Diagram interpretation questions from Ray Optics


Students should solve at least 30 to 40 important questions from each of these chapters. Use previous year papers from 2015 onward to identify recurring patterns.


Which Biology Chapters Are Most Important for NEET 2027?


Biology holds the highest weightage in NEET, with 90 questions out of 180 in the paper. Scoring well in Biology can dramatically improve your overall rank.


Top Biology Chapters for NEET 2027


Human Physiology: Digestion, Breathing, Body Fluids, Neural Control

Genetics and Evolution: Principles of Inheritance, Molecular Basis of Inheritance

Plant Physiology: Photosynthesis, Plant Growth, Mineral Nutrition

Cell Biology: Cell Structure, Cell Division

Ecology: Organisms and Populations, Ecosystem, Biodiversity

Reproduction: Human Reproduction, Reproductive Health


Common Question Patterns in Biology

Direct NCERT-based theory questions

Diagram labeling from Human Physiology and Plant Anatomy

Match the following from Genetics and Ecology

Assertion and Reasoning questions from Cell Biology


Biology preparation for NEET should always begin and end with NCERT textbooks. Every line, diagram, table, and example in NCERT is a potential question source.

Common Mistakes Students Should Avoid


Many students work hard but still miss their target score. Here are the most common mistakes to watch out for:


Ignoring NCERT: Jumping to reference books before finishing NCERT thoroughly

Skipping weak chapters: Avoiding difficult topics instead of working through them

Not taking mock tests: Practicing without timed conditions leads to poor time management on exam day

Overreliance on shortcuts: Memorizing tricks without understanding concepts

Last-minute cramming: Trying to cover the entire syllabus in the final month

Not analyzing mistakes: Solving questions without reviewing wrong answers


Students who review their errors after every practice session improve faster than those who simply solve more questions without reflection.

Frequently Asked Questions


1. How many questions are asked in NEET from Physics and Biology?

NEET has 200 questions in total, out of which students attempt 180. Physics and Biology each have two sections. Biology carries 360 marks (90 questions), and Physics carries 180 marks (45 questions).


2. Is NCERT enough for NEET Biology preparation?

Yes, NCERT is the primary and most important source for NEET Biology. Most questions are directly or indirectly based on NCERT content. Students should read every line, table, and diagram carefully.


3. How difficult is the Physics section in NEET?

Physics is considered moderately difficult. It tests both conceptual understanding and numerical problem-solving ability. Regular practice of important questions from Mechanics and Electrodynamics can help improve your score significantly.


4. When should I start preparing for NEET 2027?

Ideally, start your preparation from Class 11 itself. Beginning in early 2025 gives you over two years of preparation time, which is sufficient for thorough coverage, revision, and mock test practice.


5. What is the expected fee for MBBS in government colleges?

MBBS fees in government medical colleges typically range from ₹10,000 to ₹50,000 per year, depending on the state and institution. Private college fees can range from ₹5,00,000 to ₹25,00,000 per year.


6. Can I prepare for NEET without coaching?

Yes, many students have cleared NEET through self-study using NCERT books, previous year papers, and trusted digital learning resources. The key is consistency, a structured plan, and regular self-assessment through mock tests.
