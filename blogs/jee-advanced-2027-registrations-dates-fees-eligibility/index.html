JEE Advanced 2027 Registration: Step-by-Step Application Process, Fees, and Documents | EduRain Blog

JEE Advanced 2027 Registrations are expected to open in April 2027 on the official website, jeeadv.ac.in, for students who qualify through JEE Main 2027. The brochure has not been released yet. Candidates will need to apply online, upload documents, pay the fee, and download the confirmation page.

When Will JEE Advanced 2027 Registration Open?

The official JEE Advanced 2027 registration date has not been announced as of October 2026. Going by recent years, registration is expected in April 2027, after JEE Main results, and may close in early May.

Past cycles give a useful guide, but they are not official 2027 dates.

Who Can Apply for JEE Advanced 2027?

Eligibility for 2027 will be fixed in the official brochure. For 2026, candidates needed a rank within the top 2,50,000 in the JEE Main B.E./B. Tech paper, and had to meet the age and attempt limits.

These were the 2026 conditions:

  1. Rank within the top 2,50,000 (all categories) in the JEE Main B.E./B.Tech paper.
  2. Date of birth on or after 1 October 2001, with five years of relaxation for SC, ST, and PwD candidates.
  3. A maximum of two attempts in two consecutive years.
  4. Class 12 passed in 2025 or appearing in 2026.
  5. At least 75% aggregate in Class 12 (65% for SC, ST, and PwD) for admission to IITs.

The age and Class 12 year conditions will change for 2027. Check the new brochure before you apply.

How Does the JEE Advanced 2027 Application Process Work?

Based on the 2026 process, candidates log in on the official website, fill the form, upload documents, pay online, and download the confirmation page. The 2027 brochure will confirm the exact steps.

Here is the JEE Advanced 2027 registration process, based on the 2026 flow:

  1. Visit jeeadv.ac.in and open the registration link.
  2. Log in using your JEE Main details and verify them.
  3. Fill in personal, academic, and category details, and choose an exam city.
  4. Upload your photograph, signature, and certificates in the prescribed format.
  5. Pay the fee online by net banking, card, or UPI.
  6. Submit the form and download the confirmation page.

A limited correction window may be provided after registration closes. Do not depend on it.

What Are the JEE Advanced 2027 Registration Fees?

The 2027 fee has not been announced. In 2026, Indian centres charged ₹1,600 for female, SC, ST, and PwD candidates and ₹3,200 for all others. The fee was not refundable.

Candidate category (Indian centres, 2026)Fee
Female candidates (all categories)₹1,600
SC, ST, and PwD candidates₹1,600
All other candidates₹3,200

Foreign centres charged between USD 200 and USD 300 in 2026, depending on the country group. Payment gateway and service charges were extra. Official JEE Advanced 2027 registration fees will be published when JEE Advanced 2027 Registrations open.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

FAQ 1: Has JEE Advanced 2027 registration started?

No. As of October 2026, the brochure and dates have not been released. Registration is expected in April 2027, but this is based on past patterns, not an official notice.

FAQ 2: What is the official website for the JEE Advanced 2027 application form?

The application form is expected on jeeadv.ac.in, the official JEE Advanced website. Avoid third-party links when entering personal details or making payments.

FAQ 3: What was the registration fee in the last cycle?

For Indian centres in 2026, the fee was ₹1,600 for female, SC, ST, and PwD candidates. All other candidates paid ₹3,200. The 2027 fee will be announced in the brochure.

FAQ 4: Is JEE Advanced registration the same as JoSAA registration?

No. JEE Advanced registration is for the entrance exam. Admission counselling needs a separate registration on josaa.nic.in after results.

FAQ 5: Can a student apply without qualifying in JEE Main?

No. Only candidates who meet the JEE Main rank cut-off in the brochure are eligible. In 2026, the limit was the top 2,50,000 candidates in the B.E./B.Tech paper.

FAQ 6: Can the registration deadline be extended?

Extensions have happened before. In 2026, the closing date was extended to 5 May. Students should still apply early and not plan around a possible extension.
