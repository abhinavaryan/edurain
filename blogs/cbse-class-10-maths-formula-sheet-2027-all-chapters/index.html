This CBSE Class 10 Maths Formula Sheet 2027 lists the key formulas from all 14 NCERT chapters, from Real Numbers to Probability. Use it for quick revision, then solve NCERT questions to apply each formula. Confirm the latest syllabus on the official CBSE academic website before you begin your preparation.

How many chapters and marks does Class 10 Maths have in 2027?

The Class 10 Maths syllabus has 14 NCERT chapters. The written paper carries 80 marks and internal assessment carries 20 marks. Formulas appear in every unit, so chapter-wise revision is the most practical approach.

The 80 theory marks are split across seven units as shown below. These are syllabus marks, not a prediction of exam questions.

Which Number Systems and Algebra formulas matter most?

Real Numbers and Algebra together carry 26 marks. Students should know the HCF and LCM relation, the quadratic formula, the discriminant, and the nth term and sum of an arithmetic progression.

What are the main Mensuration formulas for circles and solids?

Mensuration covers circle measurements and the surface area and volume of common solids. Students should remember each formula with its correct units. Combined solid questions use these same formulas in sequence.

How can you revise formulas and avoid common mistakes?

Revise by writing each formula from memory, then using it in a question. Print your CBSE Class 10 Maths Formula Sheet 2027 and test yourself chapter by chapter. Most errors come from wrong substitution, not from forgotten formulas.

Follow these steps:

  1. Write the formulas of one chapter without looking.
  2. Check against NCERT and correct every gap.
  3. Solve NCERT examples, then exercise questions.
  4. Attempt CBSE sample papers under timed conditions.

Common mistakes include:

  1. Using diameter instead of radius in circle and cone questions.
  2. Forgetting that the discriminant must be calculated before judging roots.
  3. Mixing CSA and TSA for cylinders and hemispheres.
  4. Dropping the ± sign in the quadratic formula.

Try these practice questions:

  1. Find the LCM of 12 and 18. (HCF is 6, so LCM = 12 × 18 / 6 = 36.)
  2. Does 2x² − 4x + 3 = 0 have real roots? (D = 16 − 24 = −8, so no real roots.)
  3. Find the 10th term of the AP 2, 7, 12. (a₁₀ = 2 + 9 × 5 = 47.)

Key Takeaways

  1. Class 10 Maths has 14 NCERT chapters, 80 theory marks, and 20 internal marks.
  2. Algebra carries the highest unit marks, so master its formulas first.
  3. Trigonometry needs the three identities and the standard angle table.
  4. Mensuration formulas must be matched to the correct solid and units.
  5. Writing formulas from memory works better than reading them repeatedly.
  6. Always verify the syllabus on the official CBSE academic website.

Conclusion and Next Steps

A formula sheet works best as a revision tool, not a replacement for NCERT. Learn the formulas chapter by chapter, apply them in questions, and review your errors. Your next step is to pick one unit today, write its formulas from memory, and solve five NCERT questions on it.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

FAQ 1: What are the most important formulas for Class 10 Maths?

The most important formulas include the quadratic formula, the nth term and sum of an AP, the distance and section formulas, trigonometric identities, and surface area and volume formulas. Statistics formulas for mean, median, and mode are also important.

FAQ 2: Where can I download the Class 10 Maths formula sheet PDF?

This page does not host a downloadable file. You can save it as a PDF using your browser's print option. For syllabus documents, use the official CBSE academic website.

FAQ 3: How many chapters are there in Class 10 Maths CBSE 2027?

There are 14 NCERT chapters in the Class 10 Maths syllabus for the 2027 board exam. They run from Real Numbers to Probability and are grouped into seven units.

FAQ 4: How can I remember Class 10 Maths formulas easily?

Group formulas by chapter, write them from memory daily, and apply each one in a question. Make short flashcards for trigonometric values and mensuration formulas, and revise them weekly.

FAQ 5: Which trigonometry formulas are important for Class 10?

Remember the three ratios, the identities sin²θ + cos²θ = 1, 1 + tan²θ = sec²θ and 1 + cot²θ = cosec²θ, the complementary angle relations, and the standard angle values from 0° to 90°.

FAQ 6: What are the surface area and volume formulas for Class 10?

Key formulas include cylinder CSA 2πrh and volume πr²h, cone CSA πrl and volume ⅓πr²h, and sphere surface area 4πr² and volume (4/3)πr³. Hemisphere TSA is 3πr².
