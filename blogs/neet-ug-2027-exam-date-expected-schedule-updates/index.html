NEET UG 2027 Exam Date: Expected Schedule and Latest Updates | EduRain Blog

NTA has not announced the NEET UG 2027 Exam Date yet. The exam is expected in early May 2027. It will be a computer-based test. This guide separates confirmed facts from expected dates, so you can plan calmly and avoid unverified claims.

What Has NTA Officially Confirmed So Far?

As of 22 October 2026, NTA has not released the information bulletin or any date. The confirmed change is that NEET UG moves to computer-based testing from 2027, announced by the Centre on 15 May 2026.

ItemCurrent Status
Computer-based mode from 2027Announced by the Centre
Exam dateNot announced
Information bulletinNot released
NTA calendar for December 2026 to March 2027Does not list NEET UG 2027
RegistrationNot started


Treat any other date you see online as an estimate.

When Is the Exam Expected in May 2027?

NEET is expected in early May 2027, most likely on Sunday, 2 May. This estimate comes from recent years, when NEET was held on the first Sunday of May. It is not official.

The date may still change because of the shift to computer-based testing. Several reports say the exam may run over five to six days. This is reported, not official. If that happens, a single Sunday date may not apply.

Check the final date on neet.nta.nic.in once the bulletin is published.

Expected NEET 2027 Registration Date and Timeline

The NEET 2027 registration date is not announced. Past patterns suggest applications open around February 2027 and close in March 2027. NTA will confirm this in the bulletin.

How Computer-Based Testing May Affect the Schedule

Computer-based testing may change how many days the exam runs and how candidates are assigned to shifts. NTA has not published these rules. Students should wait for the bulletin before assuming any shift or day pattern.

Since the mode is changing, practise on screen early. Many students build speed and comfort through NEET 2027 Online Preparation programmes that include computer-based mock tests. Check the test interface and performance analysis before you join any programme.

Here is what students can reasonably expect to check in the bulletin:

  1. Exam dates and the number of days.
  2. Shift timings and reporting time.
  3. Exam duration and marking scheme.
  4. Rules for scoring across different shifts.
  5. Syllabus and any other rule changes for 2027.

Where to Check Verified NEET 2027 Latest News and Updates

The most reliable sources are neet.nta.nic.in and nta.ac.in. Third-party pages often publish estimates as facts. Compare any date you read with the official website before acting on it.

Follow these steps:

  1. Open neet.nta.nic.in and read the latest notices.
  2. Check nta.ac.in for the NTA exam calendar.
  3. Note the date of every update you read.
  4. Ignore claims of confirmed dates that cite no official notice.
  5. Download the information bulletin once it is released.

How to Plan Preparation Before the Notification

Start now and fix your study routine around an early May 2027 target. Treat the date as flexible. A steady plan works whether the exam falls on 2 May or a few days later.

A practical plan includes the following:

  1. Finish NCERT Biology line by line, including diagrams and tables.
  2. Divide Physics and Chemistry into weekly targets.
  3. Take a weekly timed test and review every wrong answer.
  4. Practice on computer-based mock tests to build screen comfort.
  5. Revise formulas and reactions on a fixed day each week.

Students who need structure can look at live classes, mock tests, and doubt support from online providers. When you compare the Best NEET UG 2027 Online Coaching options, avoid choices that promise ranks or guaranteed selection.

Some students prefer Online NEET Exam Preparation because it allows flexible timing. Others compare NEET Online Coaching for 2027 and 2028 to plan a longer study cycle. Whichever route you choose, a consistent schedule matters more than the platform.

Until the bulletin arrives, use the latest published NTA syllabus and NCERT books. Do not assume any syllabus change that NTA has not announced.

Key Takeaways

  1. NTA has not announced the exam date or released the information bulletin.
  2. Computer-based testing from 2027 is the main confirmed change.
  3. The NEET UG 2027 Exam Date is expected in early May 2027, but this is unofficial.
  4. Registration is expected around February and March 2027, based on past patterns.
  5. Reports of a five- to six-day exam are unconfirmed.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

FAQ 1: When will NTA announce the NEET UG 2027 date?

NTA has not announced it. Many sources estimate the bulletin around February 2027, based on earlier years. Only neet.nta.nic.in can confirm the official date and registration schedule.

FAQ 2: Will NEET UG 2027 be a computer-based test?

Yes. The Centre announced the move to computer-based testing from 2027. NTA is expected to explain shifts, duration, and rules in the information bulletin.

FAQ 3: Will NEET 2027 be held in one day?

This is not confirmed. Some reports suggest the exam may run over five to six days in multiple shifts. Students should wait for the official bulletin before assuming a single day.

FAQ 4: When may NEET 2027 registration begin?

Registration is expected around February 2027, with the last date in March 2027. These are estimates based on earlier patterns. NTA will confirm the dates in the bulletin.

FAQ 5: Should students wait for the official date before starting preparation?

No. The syllabus and study routine do not depend on the date. Start NCERT revision, weekly tests, and computer-based practice now, and adjust the plan once NTA confirms the schedule.

FAQ 6: Can the expected 2 May date change?

Yes. The 2 May estimate comes from the first Sunday pattern of earlier years. The shift to computer-based testing may change the date or spread the exam over several days.
