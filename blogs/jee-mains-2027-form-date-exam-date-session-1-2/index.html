Every JEE aspirant tracks two dates above all else: when the form opens and when the exam begins. JEE Mains 2027 Overview

JEE Mains 2027 will be conducted by the National Testing Agency across two sessions, following the same structure used in recent years. NTA released its academic calendar on 16 September 2026, confirming the Session 1 exam window while leaving Session 2 dates and the full bulletin pending.

Session 1 exam dates are officially confirmed as 22 to 24 January and 28 to 30 January 2027, with 31 January kept as a buffer day. Session 2 is expected in April 2027, though NTA has not released those exact dates yet. Treat every Session 2 figure below as a projection until the official bulletin arrives.

How the Application Form Process Works

Filling the form correctly matters as much as knowing the JEE Main 2027 Form Date & Exam Date. Follow these steps once NTA opens registration.

  1. Visit the official NTA JEE Main portal only, never a third-party site.
  2. Complete new registration using your name exactly as it appears on your Class 10 marksheet.
  3. Fill in personal details, academic qualification, category, and paper choice carefully.
  4. Select up to four exam city preferences in order of priority.
  5. Upload a photograph and signature according to the specified size and format.
  6. Pay the application fee before the deadline and save the confirmation page.
  7. Recheck every entry during the correction window before it closes.

Common Mistakes Students Make During Form Filling and Date Tracking

Small errors during registration cause real problems later, from mismatched documents to missed deadlines. Most of these mistakes are avoidable with careful attention during the form process.

  1. Entering a name that does not match the Class 10 certificate exactly.
  2. Waiting until the last day to pay the application fee and missing the deadline.
  3. Ignoring the correction window and carrying an error into the admit card.
  4. Confusing Session 1 and Session 2 dates while planning revision schedules.
  5. Relying on unofficial websites for date updates instead of the NTA portal.
  6. Skipping the exam city preference step and receiving a distant allotted center.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. What are the confirmed JEE Mains 2027 Session 1 exam dates?

Session 1 is confirmed for 22 to 24 January and 28 to 30 January 2027, with 31 January kept as a buffer day.

2. When is the JEE Mains 2027 application form expected to open?

The Session 1 form is expected to open in November 2026, though NTA has not released the official bulletin yet.

3. Has NTA announced JEE Mains 2027 Session 2 dates?

No, Session 2 dates remain projected for April 2027 and will be confirmed through a separate NTA bulletin later.

4. Where should students check official JEE Mains 2027 dates?

Always check jeemain.nta.nic.in directly, since third-party sites can carry outdated or incorrect projections.

5. What happens if the correction window is missed after form submission?

Errors carried past the correction window usually stay on the admit card, so candidates should review entries carefully before it closes.

6. Can a candidate appear for both JEE Mains 2027 sessions?

Yes, candidates may attempt both sessions and their best score across attempts is considered for the final result.
