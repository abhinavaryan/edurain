CBSE Class 10th Science Most Important & Expected Questions for Board Exam 2027 | EduRain Blog

Students looking for CBSE Class 10 Science Important Questions need a list tied to the 2027 paper, not random practice. This guide covers the confirmed exam schedule, chapters to prioritise, expected questions with answer points, key formulas, and common mistakes. Expected questions are based on the syllabus and past patterns, not on any official announcement.

Latest Exam Pattern and Official Updates for 2027

CBSE will hold the Class 10 Phase 1 board exams from 16 February to 9 March 2027. Phase 2 is optional and expected in May 2027. Science carries 80 marks in theory and 20 marks in internal assessment.

Phase 1 is compulsory for everyone. Phase 2 is meant for students who want to improve their marks in selected subjects. Reports on the 2026 to 2027 sample papers say the question paper design and marking scheme have not changed.

Check the exact Science paper date, timing, and subject code on the official CBSE date sheet at cbse.gov.in. Download the sample paper and marking scheme from cbseacademic.nic.in. Everything above is official or officially reported. The questions listed below are expected.

Important Chapters to Prioritise in Class 10 Science

All three branches of Science carry marks, so no chapter should be skipped. Chemical Reactions, Acids, Bases and Salts, Carbon Compounds, Life Processes, Heredity, Light, Electricity and Magnetic Effects have appeared frequently in past papers.

How to Use Expected Questions in Your Preparation

Solve expected questions only after reading the NCERT chapter. Attempt them in timed sets, check your answers against the official marking scheme, and revise every mistake within a week. This turns a question list into real exam practice.

  1. Read the NCERT chapter and underline definitions, reactions, and diagrams.
  2. Solve the NCERT in-text and exercise questions first.
  3. Attempt expected questions in a timed set of ten to fifteen.
  4. Compare your answers with the marking scheme and note the missed keywords.
  5. Revisit weak chapters every Sunday with a short test.

Self-study works best when it is backed by regular doubt-solving and weekly tests. Common Mistakes Students Make in Science Board Answers

Most lost marks come from avoidable errors, not from missing knowledge. Unlabelled diagrams, unbalanced equations, missing units, and overly long answers for short questions are the mistakes teachers see most often.

  1. Leaving diagrams unlabelled or drawing them without arrows.
  2. Writing chemical equations without balancing or state symbols where required.
  3. Skipping units and sign conventions in numericals.
  4. Writing a paragraph for a two-mark question instead of two clear points.
  5. Ignoring case-based questions until the last week.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

FAQ 1: What are the most important questions for CBSE Class 10 Science 2027?

Definitions, balanced equations, labelled diagrams, ray diagrams, and standard numericals from NCERT are the most useful. Practise questions on Chemical Reactions, Carbon Compounds, Life Processes, Heredity, Light and Electricity first, then move to the remaining chapters.

FAQ 2: Which questions are expected in the Class 10 Science Board Exam 2027?

No one can confirm the exact paper. Expected questions follow the syllabus and past patterns, such as the nephron, reflex arc, Mendel's cross, circuit numericals, and soap action. Use them as practice, not as a guarantee.

FAQ 3: What are the most repeated questions in CBSE Class 10 Science?

Frequently repeated topics include balancing equations, properties of acids and bases, the human eye defects, parallel and series circuits, and the F2 ratio. Check previous year papers on the official website to see how these topics appeared.

FAQ 4: Which chapters are important for CBSE Class 10 Science 2027?

All prescribed chapters matter because each unit carries marks. Give extra revision time to Chemical Reactions, Acids, Bases and Salts, Life Processes, Heredity, Light and Electricity. Always confirm the final list in the latest CBSE syllabus.

FAQ 5: How to prepare for the CBSE Class 10 Science Board Exam 2027?

Read NCERT line by line, solve exercises, then attempt timed question sets and sample papers. Review each answer against the marking scheme, practise diagrams daily, and revise formulas weekly. Take Phase 1 seriously, since Phase 2 is only an optional improvement attempt.

FAQ 6: What are the most expected questions in Class 10 Science?

Short answer questions on definitions and reactions, long answers on the nephron, reproduction and ray diagrams, and numericals on electricity and light are strong candidates. Case-based questions from any chapter are also worth practising.
