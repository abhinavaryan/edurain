Top 40 Most Repeated PYQs in Cell Biology & Biomolecules for NEET | EduRain Blog

Cell Biology and Biomolecules are two of the highest-scoring chapters in the NEET Biology section. Every year, 8 to 12 questions appear from these topics, making them essential for every serious aspirant. If you are preparing for NEET 2025 or beyond, understanding previous year questions (PYQs) from these chapters can give you a significant advantage.

This blog is for NEET aspirants who want to focus on the most repeated questions from Cell Biology and Biomolecules. You will learn which concepts are tested again and again, how to approach them, and what strategies will help you score full marks in these chapters.


What Topics Fall Under Cell Biology and Biomolecules for NEET?


Cell Biology covers the structure and function of cells, cell organelles, cell division, and the cell cycle. Biomolecules cover the chemistry of carbohydrates, proteins, lipids, nucleic acids, and enzymes.


NEET follows the NCERT syllabus prescribed by NTA. The key chapters include:


  1. Cell: The Unit of Life (Class 11)
  2. Cell Cycle and Cell Division (Class 11)
  3. Biomolecules (Class 11)
  4. Transport in Cells (selected concepts)


These chapters are straightforward if you study NCERT thoroughly. Most PYQs are direct NCERT picks, which means memorizing key lines and diagrams from the textbook is non-negotiable.


Why Are PYQs So Important for NEET Preparation?


PYQs reveal the exact pattern NTA follows. Solving them helps you understand which facts, definitions, and diagrams get repeated.


Here is why every topper recommends PYQ practice:


  1. Pattern Recognition: You notice that certain facts about mitosis, meiosis, enzyme classification, and amino acids appear every 2 to 3 years.
  2. Confidence Building: Solving real exam questions builds familiarity with the difficulty level.
  3. Time Management: Repeated practice helps you solve questions faster during the actual exam.
  4. Revision Tool: PYQs act as a focused revision sheet for last-minute preparation.

Students who study the cell cycle expect questions with solutions before the exam and often find that 3 to 5 questions in their paper come from topics they have already practiced.


Most Repeated PYQs in Cell Biology for NEET


Cell Biology is a goldmine for scoring easy marks. NTA has repeatedly asked questions from the following areas. Below is a categorized list of the 20 most repeated question types from this unit.


Cell Organelles and Their Functions


  1. Which organelle is called the "powerhouse of the cell"?
  2. What is the function of the Golgi apparatus?
  3. Difference between smooth ER and rough ER.
  4. Which organelle contains its own DNA? (Mitochondria and Chloroplast)
  5. Structure and function of lysosomes.
  6. What is the fluid mosaic model of the plasma membrane?
  7. Role of ribosomes in protein synthesis.
  8. Functions of the cytoskeleton.


Cell Cycle and Cell Division


  1. Name the phases of the cell cycle and their duration.
  2. What happens during the S phase of interphase?
  3. Difference between mitosis and meiosis.
  4. At which stage do chromosomes align at the metaphase plate?
  5. What is crossing over, and during which phase does it occur?
  6. Significance of meiosis in organisms.
  7. How does cytokinesis differ in plant and animal cells?
  8. What are checkpoints in the cell cycle?
  9. Explain the stages of prophase I in meiosis.
  10. Role of spindle fibers during cell division.
  11. Why is mitosis called equational division?
  12. What is the significance of the G0 phase?


These questions appear so frequently that practicing cell cycle questions with solutions from the last 15 years will cover almost every possible variation NTA can ask.

Common Mistake: Students often confuse the events of Prophase I substages (Leptotene, Zygotene, Pachytene, Diplotene, Diakinesis). Make a mnemonic and revise it weekly.


Most Repeated PYQs in Biomolecules for NEET


Biomolecules questions in NEET are mostly definition-based and concept-based. NTA frequently picks lines directly from NCERT Chapter 9 of Class 11 Biology.


Carbohydrates and Lipids

  1. What is the general formula of carbohydrates?
  2. Give examples of monosaccharides, disaccharides, and polysaccharides.
  3. What is glycosidic bond?
  4. Difference between starch and cellulose.
  5. What are saturated and unsaturated fatty acids?


Proteins and Amino Acids

  1. How many types of amino acids are found in proteins?
  2. What is a peptide bond?
  3. Explain primary, secondary, tertiary, and quaternary structures of proteins.
  4. What is denaturation of proteins?
  5. Give examples of fibrous and globular proteins.


Enzymes

  1. What are enzymes? Why are they called biological catalysts?
  2. Explain the lock and key model of enzyme action.
  3. What is the induced fit model?
  4. Factors affecting enzyme activity (temperature, pH, substrate concentration).
  5. What are coenzymes and cofactors?


Nucleic Acids

  1. Difference between DNA and RNA.
  2. What are the types of RNA?
  3. What is the structure of a nucleotide?
  4. Explain Chargaff's rule.
  5. What are the bases found in DNA and RNA?


Practical Tip: For Biomolecules, make a comparison table for DNA vs RNA, enzymes vs hormones, and different types of carbohydrates. Tables make revision 5 times faster.


How to Solve These PYQs Effectively


Simply reading PYQs is not enough. You need a strategy to extract maximum benefit.


Follow this step-by-step approach:

  1. Read NCERT First: Complete the chapter from NCERT before touching any PYQ.
  2. Attempt Without Hints: Solve each PYQ on your own before checking the answer.
  3. Mark Weak Areas: If you get a question wrong, highlight that topic and revisit the NCERT paragraph.
  4. Revise Weekly: Go through solved PYQs every Sunday as part of your weekly revision cycle.
  5. Simulate Exam Conditions: Set a timer and solve 40 PYQs in 30 minutes to build speed.


Many online education platforms now offer chapter-wise PYQ sets with detailed explanations, making it easier to practice from home. Edurain is one such platform where students can find well-organized study resources for competitive exams.



What Career Scope Does Strong Biology Knowledge Offer?


Scoring well in Cell Biology and Biomolecules does more than just boost your NEET rank. It builds a foundation for future studies in medicine, biotechnology, genetics, and biochemistry.


After clearing NEET, students can pursue:

  1. MBBS / BDS from government or private medical colleges
  2. BAMS / BHMS for alternative medicine pathways
  3. B.Sc. in Biotechnology, Microbiology, or Biochemistry
  4. Research careers through institutions recognized by UGC and CSIR


The salary for medical professionals starts from INR 6 to 10 LPA for fresh MBBS graduates and increases significantly with specialization. Biotechnology graduates can expect starting salaries of INR 3 to 5 LPA, with growth potential in pharma and research sectors.

Important Note: Salary and placement figures vary based on institution, location, and individual performance. No fixed outcome is guaranteed.

FAQs

1. How many questions come from Cell Biology and Biomolecules in NEET?

On average, 8 to 12 questions appear from these two chapters combined. This makes them among the highest weightage topics in the Biology section.


2. Is NCERT enough to solve PYQs from these chapters?

Yes. About 90% of NEET Biology questions, including Cell Biology and Biomolecules, are based directly on NCERT textbook content. Read every line carefully and memorize key definitions.


3. How difficult are Cell Cycle and Cell Division questions in NEET?

Most questions are moderate in difficulty. They test factual knowledge about phases of mitosis, meiosis, and events during the cell cycle. Consistent NCERT revision makes them easy to answer.


4. What is the best way to memorize Biomolecules for NEET?

Use comparison tables, flowcharts, and mnemonics. For example, remember the substitutes of Prophase I using "Lazy Zebras Play During Dawn" for Leptotene, Zygotene, Pachytene, Diplotene, and Diakinesis.


5. Are there any fees for accessing NEET PYQs online?

NTA provides official question papers for free on its website. Many educational platforms also offer free PYQ sets. Some platforms charge for detailed video solutions and analytics.


6. What salary can I expect after clearing NEET and completing MBBS?

Fresh MBBS graduates typically earn INR 6 to 10 LPA in government hospitals. Salaries increase with specialization (MD/MS). Private practice income varies widely based on location and experience. No fixed salary is guaranteed.
