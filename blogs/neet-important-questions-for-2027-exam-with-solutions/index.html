Physics: Important Questions with Solutions

Physics questions in NEET test formula application and unit handling under time pressure, often blending mechanics with basic calculations.

Question 1: A body of mass 2 kg moving at 10 metre per second is brought to rest in 5 seconds. Find the force applied.

Solution: Acceleration equals change in velocity divided by time, which is negative 10 divided by 5, giving negative 2 metre per second squared. Force equals mass times acceleration, so 2 times negative 2 equals negative 4 N. The magnitude of force applied is 4 N.

Question 2: Find the equivalent resistance of two resistors of 4 ohm and 6 ohm connected in parallel.

Solution: For parallel resistors, one over R equals one over 4 plus one over 6. This equals 3 over 12 plus 2 over 12, giving 5 over 12. So R equals 12 divided by 5, which is 2.4 ohms.

Chemistry: Important Questions with Solutions

Chemistry questions in NEET combine Physical, Organic, and Inorganic concepts, testing both formula recall and reaction understanding.

Question 3: Calculate the pH of a solution with hydrogen ion concentration equal to 1 times 10 to the power negative 4 mol per litre.

Solution: pH equals the negative log of the hydrogen ion concentration. Since the concentration is 10 to the power negative 4, the pH equals 4.

Question 4: Identify the hybridization of the central carbon atom in ethene, C2H4.

Solution: Each carbon atom in ethene forms three sigma bonds and one pi bond, using three hybrid orbitals. This corresponds to sp2 hybridization for each carbon atom in the molecule.

Biology: Important Questions with Solutions

Biology questions in NEET carry the highest weightage and often test precise NCERT terminology alongside conceptual understanding.

Question 5: Name the enzyme responsible for unwinding the DNA double helix during replication, and state its function.

Solution: The enzyme is DNA helicase. It breaks the hydrogen bonds between complementary base pairs, separating the two strands so replication machinery can access the template.

Question 6: During human respiration, explain why oxygen diffuses from alveoli into blood capillaries.

Solution: Oxygen diffuses due to a partial pressure gradient, since alveolar oxygen pressure is higher than in deoxygenated blood. Gas movement follows this gradient until pressures equalize across the alveolar membrane.

Common Mistakes Students Make While Solving These Questions

Many students lose marks on these exact question types due to avoidable errors rather than lack of preparation.

  1. Confusing similar-sounding terms in Biology, such as diffusion and osmosis.
  2. Skipping unit conversion in Physics numericals, leading to wrong final values.
  3. Misapplying formulas in pH and equilibrium questions under Chemistry.
  4. Writing incomplete reasoning in Biology questions that need short explanations.
  5. Rushing through multi-step Chemistry reactions without checking each intermediate step.

Reviewing each mistake after solving builds real accuracy across all three subjects.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are these important questions enough to cover the full NEET 2027 syllabus?

How should students approach numerical questions in NEET Physics and Chemistry?

Write down given values with correct units before applying any formula. This prevents calculation errors that come from rushing into substitution.
