NTA JEE Main 2027 Aadhaar Guidelines: Important Rules, Documents & Registration Details | EduRain Blog

Before filling the JEE Main 2027 Session 1 form, NTA advises you to complete Aadhaar re-KYC through DigiLocker. You should log in with your own credentials and keep your name and date of birth identical to your Class 10 records. Following the NTA JEE Main 2027 Aadhaar Guidelines helps you avoid identity and biometric problems later.

This guide explains the advisory, the Re-KYC process, documents, nationality categories, and key dates.

What Do the Latest NTA Instructions Say About Aadhaar?

NTA advises applicants to sign in with their own DigiLocker, JanParichay, or ePramaan (MeriPehchaan) account. As part of the NTA JEE Main 2027 Aadhaar Guidelines, applicants are also advised to complete Aadhaar Re-KYC on DigiLocker before applying, ensuring that their latest details are fetched.

Although the advisory says "advised" rather than "compulsory", candidates should complete this verification before submitting their application. Identifying a mismatch later may make corrections more difficult than completing a few minutes of verification in advance.

Here is how confirmed and unconfirmed points separate:

  1. Confirmed in published reports of the advisory: own login, Re KYC before the form, and details matching Class 10 records.
  2. Not confirmed: any extra Aadhaar step NTA may announce later.

Track NTA JEE Main 2027 Latest Updates on jeemain.nta.nic.in. This article summarises published reports and does not replace the official Information Bulletin.

How Do Candidates Complete Aadhaar Re-KYC on DigiLocker?

Open DigiLocker, go to Issued Documents, choose Refresh from the three-dot menu on your Aadhaar card, give consent, and enter the OTP sent to your Aadhaar-linked mobile. This fetches your current UIDAI details.

Follow these steps for DigiLocker Re-KYC for JEE Main 2027:

  1. Open the DigiLocker website or mobile app.
  2. Log in with your personal account.
  3. Go to Issued Documents and tap the three-dot menu on the Aadhaar card.
  4. Select Refresh, give consent, and enter the OTP.
  5. Check that your name, date of birth, and photograph look correct.

Re-KYC only fetches what UIDAI holds today. If your Aadhaar photo or name is outdated, update it through UIDAI first. Start early, because UIDAI updates take time.

Which Login and Name Matching Rules Prevent Registration Errors?

Always use your own DigiLocker, JanParichay, or ePramaan credentials, never a parent's or an operator's. Your name, parents' details, and date of birth must match the Class 10 certificate or marksheet.

These JEE Main 2027 Registration Guidelines point to common mistakes:

  1. Using someone else's login: At an internet cafe or common service center, make sure the operator uses your personal DigiLocker.
  2. Skipping Re-KYC: Recent Aadhaar changes may not appear without a refresh.
  3. Typing from memory: Copy spellings and dates directly from the Class 10 marksheet.
  4. Ignoring initials and spacing: Small differences in a name can trigger verification trouble.

What Documents and Details Should Students Keep Ready?

Keep your Class 10 certificate or marksheet, your Aadhaar-linked mobile number, access to your own DigiLocker, and a device that can capture a live photograph. Confirm the full document list and file specifications in the Information Bulletin.

Reports on the advisory mention that the bulletin requires a live photograph during the form. Keep your camera working and your face clearly visible. The JEE Main Aadhaar DigiLocker Verification flow runs smoothly when everything is ready before you begin.

How Do Nationality Categories and Non-Aadhaar Cases Work?

NTA lists three categories: Indian Nationals (including NRIs with Aadhaar), Foreign Nationals (including OCI cardholders), and NRIs Having No Aadhaar (Indian passport holders without Aadhaar). Select the one matching your citizenship and Aadhaar status.

CategoryWho it covers
Indian NationalsIndian citizens, including NRIs who hold an Aadhaar card
Foreign NationalsForeign citizens and OCI cardholders
NRIs Having No AadhaarNRIs with an Indian passport but no Aadhaar

These JEE Main 2027 Aadhaar Card Rules matter most for applicants living abroad. A wrong category can cause avoidable verification issues.

For reference, in the 2026 cycle, NTA asked some applicants who used a PAN card or a passport to upload a Photo Verification Certificate. They also had to carry a printed copy to the exam centre. That was a 2026 requirement, so check whether the 2027 bulletin repeats it.

Session 1 registration opened on 24 October 2026 and closes on 22 November 2026 at 11:50 PM. Session 2 registration is listed from 5 January to 3 February 2027. Verify every date on the official website.

Key Takeaways

  1. Complete Aadhaar Re-KYC on DigiLocker before opening the application form.
  2. Use your own DigiLocker, JanParichay or ePramaan login.
  3. Match your name, parents' details, and date of birth with Class 10 records.
  4. Update outdated Aadhaar details through UIDAI before Re-KYC.
  5. Choose the nationality category that fits your citizenship and Aadhaar status.
  6. Session 1 registration closes on 22 November 2026 at 11:50 PM.

Registration success depends on clean identity details. In short, the NTA JEE Main 2027 Aadhaar Guidelines ask you to refresh Aadhaar data, use your own login, and match official records.

Your next step is simple. Complete Re-KYC today, compare your details with the Class 10 marksheet, and then fill the form. After submission, return to your study plan. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

FAQ 1: Is Aadhaar Re KYC compulsory for JEE Main 2027 registration?

NTA has advised Re KYC through DigiLocker before you fill the form. The wording is "advised", but completing it is the safest route to avoid mismatches.

FAQ 2: Can I use a parent's DigiLocker account to apply?

No. NTA advises candidates to use their own credentials. Another person's account can cause discrepancies in identity verification and biometric data later.

FAQ 3: Which details must match my Class 10 records?

Your name, your parents' details, and your date of birth in the application form must match the Class 10 certificate or marksheet.

FAQ 4: What if my Aadhaar photo or name is outdated?

Re-KYC fetches only what UIDAI holds. Update the details through UIDAI first, then refresh your Aadhaar in DigiLocker.

FAQ 5: Which category should an NRI without Aadhaar choose?

NRIs with an Indian passport but no Aadhaar should select "NRIs Having No Aadhaar". NRIs who hold an Aadhaar card should choose "Indian Nationals".

FAQ 6: What is the last date for Session 1 registration?

The last date is 22 November 2026, up to 11:50 PM. A correction window of 27 to 29 November 2026 has been reported. Confirm on jeemain.nta.nic.in.
