CBSE Class 12 Maths Most Repeated Questions in Board Exams | EduRain Blog

The CBSE Class 12 Maths Most Repeated Questions mostly come from Calculus, Matrices and Determinants, Vectors, Three-Dimensional Geometry, Probability, and Linear Programming. Calculus alone carries 35 of the 80 theory marks, so integrals, differential equations, and applications of derivatives deserve the earliest practice before the 2027 board examination.

The recurring patterns below come from past paper trends and are not official predictions. Confirm marks and syllabus on the CBSE website.

Which Chapters Matter Most in Class 12 Maths for 2027?

Calculus is the most important unit, carrying 35 of 80 theory marks. Vectors and Three-Dimensional Geometry follows with 14 marks, then Algebra with 10, Relations and Functions with 8, Probability with 8, and Linear Programming with 5.

Vectors and Three-Dimensional Geometry14High
Algebra10High

Total theory80

The theory paper is 80 marks, and internal assessment adds 20 marks, making 100 in all. Use this table to divide study time, and begin with Calculus when working through Class 12 Maths chapter-wise important questions.

Which Calculus Questions Appear Again and Again?

Integration problems, differential equations, application of derivatives, and continuity with differentiability appear almost every year in some form. Practise standard methods rather than single questions, because functions and numbers change between papers.

How Can Students Find Reliable Practice Material for Class 12 Maths?

Reliable practice material follows the latest NCERT syllabus, explains each step in the solution, and includes board-style questions. Students should combine NCERT exercises, CBSE sample papers, and previous year questions rather than depend on one source.

A good set of CBSE Class 12 Mathematics preparation resources starts with the NCERT textbook, because many board questions are based on its solved examples. Students who also want guided lessons and chapter-wise revision can explore the Edurain Online Education Platform alongside NCERT, since structured explanations help when a concept stays unclear from the textbook alone. After that, sample papers and previous year questions show how the board frames its questions and awards marks.

What Repeated Question Types Come From Algebra, Vectors and Three-Dimensional Geometry?

Matrices and determinants questions, such as finding an inverse and solving linear equations, are regular. Questions on dot product, cross product, shortest distance, and the angle between lines also recur in Vectors and Three-Dimensional Geometry.

What Probability and Linear Programming Questions Can Students Expect?

Probability questions usually test conditional probability, Bayes' theorem, and probability distributions. Linear Programming questions ask students to graph constraints, mark the feasible region, and find the maximum or minimum value of an objective function at corner points.

Conditional ProbabilityFind P(A given B)P(A | B) = P(A ∩ B) / P(B)
Bayes' TheoremIdentify the source of an eventP(Eᵢ | A) = P(Eᵢ) P(A | Eᵢ) / Σ P(Eⱼ) P(A | Eⱼ)

Practice question: A bag has 3 red and 4 black balls, and a second bag has 5 red and 2 black balls. A ball drawn at random from a randomly chosen bag is red. Find the probability that it came from the first bag.

How Should Students Prepare for the Class 12 Maths Board Exam 2027?

Students should finish the syllabus first, then practise chapter-wise questions, and then solve full sample papers under timed conditions. Revision should focus on formulas, derivations, and weak chapters, with regular error tracking.

StepTaskOutput
1Solve every NCERT example and exerciseConcept base

Case-based questions need extra attention. Read the passage twice and answer each subpart separately.

Which Common Mistakes Cost Marks in Class 12 Maths?

Most lost marks come from sign errors, missing constants of integration, wrong limits, and skipped steps. Weak graph work in Linear Programming and careless matrix calculations also reduce scores in an otherwise well-prepared paper.

Key Takeaways

  1. Calculus carries 35 of 80 theory marks and needs the most practice.
  2. Repeated questions are patterns from past papers, not guaranteed questions.
  3. Matrix inverse, Bayes' theorem, and shortest distance are frequent formula-based questions.
  4. NCERT examples, sample papers, and previous year questions together form a complete practice set.
  5. Timed full papers build speed and expose weak chapters early.

Conclusion and Next Steps

Strong results in Class 12 Maths come from understanding why a method works, then practising it across question types. Use the CBSE Class 12 Maths Most Repeated Questions in the tables above as a practice checklist, and attempt one timed sample paper this week to measure your starting point.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

FAQ 1: Which are the most repeated questions in CBSE Class 12 Maths?

Integration, differential equations, application of derivatives, matrix inverse, Bayes' theorem, shortest distance between lines, and Linear Programming appear often. These are patterns from past papers, so exact questions may change.

FAQ 2: What are the most important questions for the Class 12 Maths board exam 2027?

Focus on Calculus, matrices and determinants, Vectors, Three-Dimensional Geometry, and Probability. Solve NCERT examples, sample papers, and previous year papers for each unit.

FAQ 3: Which chapters are important in Class 12 Maths 2027?

Calculus is the most important, with 35 marks. Vectors and Three-Dimensional Geometry carries 14 marks, Algebra 10, Relations and Functions 8, Probability 8, and Linear Programming 5.

FAQ 4: How many marks does the Class 12 Maths theory paper carry?

The theory paper carries 80 marks, and internal assessment carries 20 marks, making a total of 100. Check the official CBSE syllabus for the latest details.

FAQ 5: How can students practise case-based questions?

Start with case-based questions in CBSE sample papers. Read the passage twice, identify the concept being tested, and answer each subpart with clear steps.

FAQ 6: Should students solve only repeated questions for the board exam?

No. Repeated questions help with focus, but the paper can include new problems. Complete the NCERT syllabus first, then use repeated patterns to guide revision.
