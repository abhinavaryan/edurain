Biology: Biotechnology and Its Applications – Complete Diagrams for NEET | EduRain Blog

Biotechnology is one of the most important chapters in Class 12 Biology and carries significant weight in the NEET exam. Every year, multiple questions appear from this unit, making it essential for serious aspirants. Biotechnology and Its Applications covers real-world uses of genetic engineering, gene therapy, transgenic organisms, and biopiracy. This article is designed for NEET aspirants who want a clear, structured understanding of the chapter along with diagram-based learning tips. You will learn what the chapter includes, which diagrams matter most, how to prepare effectively, and what career doors this knowledge can open.

What Does Biotechnology and Its Applications Cover?

This chapter deals with the practical uses of biotechnology in agriculture, medicine, and industry. It is part of Unit 10 in the NCERT Class 12 Biology syllabus prescribed by CBSE.

Key topics covered include:

  1. Bt crops such as Bt cotton and Bt brinjal
  2. Gene therapy and its use in treating ADA deficiency
  3. Transgenic animals and their applications in research
  4. Genetically modified organisms (GMOs) and their role in food production
  5. RNA interference (RNAi) as a method to silence specific genes
  6. Biopiracy and related ethical issues
  7. Insulin production through recombinant DNA technology

Students must understand both the theory and the labeled diagrams from NCERT to answer questions accurately in NEET.

Which Diagrams Are Most Important for NEET?

Diagrams play a major role in memorizing biological processes and scoring well. NEET frequently tests visual understanding of biotechnology concepts.

Here are the must-know diagrams from this chapter:

  1. Bt cotton mechanism: Shows how Cry proteins (Cry1Ac, Cry2Ab) are produced and how they kill bollworm larvae by creating pores in the gut lining
  2. Gene therapy for ADA deficiency: Illustrates the process of inserting a functional ADA gene into lymphocytes using a retroviral vector
  3. Recombinant insulin production: Depicts how proinsulin is produced in E. coli and processed into mature insulin (A and B chains)
  4. RNA interference (RNAi): Shows how double-stranded RNA silences mRNA to prevent translation of specific genes
  5. Transgenic animal diagram: Explains how foreign genes are introduced at the zygote stage

Practice drawing and labeling these diagrams at least three to four times during your revision. This helps in both MCQ elimination and assertion-reasoning questions.

How to Prepare This Chapter Effectively?

A focused approach saves time and improves retention. Here is a step-by-step method that works for most NEET aspirants:

  1. Read NCERT thoroughly: Every line from the NCERT textbook is important. NEET setters pick statements directly from it.
  2. Highlight key terms: Words such as Cry proteins, Rosie (transgenic cow), Humulin, and Agrobacterium are frequently tested.
  3. Draw diagrams daily: Spend 10 to 15 minutes drawing one diagram per day from this chapter.
  4. Solve previous year questions: At least 5 to 8 questions have appeared from Biotechnology and Its Applications in NEET papers over the past decade.
  5. Use flashcards for revision: Write one concept per card and revise them before sleeping.
  6. Join a reliable coaching program: Enrolling in the best coaching for NEET helps you get expert guidance, structured study plans, and regular mock tests.

Avoid memorizing without understanding. Focus on why a process happens, not just what happens.

What Are Common Mistakes Students Make?

Many students lose marks in this chapter due to avoidable errors. Being aware of these mistakes can improve your score.

  1. Confusing Cry1Ac and Cry2Ab with Cry1Ab (which is used in corn, not cotton)
  2. Mixing up gene therapy with genetic engineering
  3. Ignoring ethical issues related to GMOs and biopiracy, which appear in assertion-reasoning questions
  4. Not practicing diagrams and relying only on text-based revision
  5. Overlooking the RNAi mechanism, which has been asked multiple times since 2018

A common tip from toppers is to maintain a separate notebook for diagrams and short notes from this chapter. This becomes your go-to resource during the final revision week.

What Is the Career Scope After Studying Biotechnology?

Understanding biotechnology at the NEET level opens doors to multiple career paths beyond medicine. Students who develop a strong interest in this subject often explore the following options:

  1. MBBS with specialization in genetics or molecular medicine
  2. B.Tech or B.Sc in Biotechnology from institutions approved by AICTE or UGC
  3. Research positions at organizations such as ICMR, DBT, and CSIR
  4. Careers in pharmaceutical and biotech companies
  5. Bioethics and patent law for students interested in policy

Salary in biotechnology-related careers ranges from ₹3 lakh to ₹12 lakh per annum at the entry level, depending on the degree and organization. Senior researchers and specialists earn significantly more over time.

Students should note that no institution or coaching center can promise guaranteed placement or salary. Always verify claims through official sources.

Why Is This Chapter High Scoring for NEET?

Biotechnology and Its Applications is considered a high-scoring chapter because the content is straightforward, factual, and diagram-based. There is less room for confusion compared to chapters such as Genetics or Ecology.

Reasons why students find it scoring:

  1. Most questions are direct and fact-based
  2. Limited scope for tricky or calculation-heavy problems
  3. Diagrams and processes repeat frequently across years
  4. Short chapter length makes it easy to revise multiple times

Students who dedicate even two to three focused study sessions to this chapter can confidently attempt all related questions in the exam.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q1. How many questions come from Biotechnology and Its Applications in NEET?

Typically, 3 to 5 questions appear from this chapter every year. Some years have seen up to 6 questions when combined with Biotechnology Principles and Processes.

Q2. Is this chapter difficult for average students?

No. It is one of the easier chapters in Biology because the content is factual and based on specific processes. Regular reading and diagram practice make it manageable for everyone.

Q3. Which book should I study for this chapter?

NCERT Class 12 Biology is the primary and most important source. You may supplement it with previous year question papers and coaching material.

Q4. How long does it take to complete this chapter?

Most students can complete the chapter in 4 to 6 hours of focused study. Revision takes about 1 to 2 hours per session after the initial reading.

Q5. Are diagrams asked directly in NEET from this chapter?

Yes. Questions related to the Bt toxin mechanism, gene therapy process, and insulin production often include diagram-based options or require visual understanding.

Q6. What is the expected salary in biotechnology careers after NEET?

Entry-level salaries in biotechnology range from ₹3 lakh to ₹12 lakh per annum. This varies based on the role, degree, employer, and location. Students pursuing MBBS with a genetics specialization may earn more over time.
