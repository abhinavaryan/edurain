NEET UG 2027 Registration: Dates, Steps, Fees & Documents

NTA has not published the 2027 bulletin yet. The 2026 form opened on 8 February on the NTA official website. Candidates should expect NEET UG 2027 Registration to follow a similar online process. Applicants registered online, filled in details, uploaded images and certificates, paid the fee, and downloaded the confirmation page. Details may change.

NEET UG is the national entrance test for MBBS, BDS, and other undergraduate medical courses. A small error in the application can cause delays or rejection. This guide explains the expected timeline, the step-by-step process, fees, documents, and common mistakes. Every figure comes from the 2026 cycle unless stated otherwise.

How do you complete the NEET UG application step by step?

The application is fully online on the NTA official website. Candidates register, fill in personal and academic details, upload documents, pay the fee, and download the confirmation page. These steps follow the 2026 process and may change in 2027.

  1. Register. Open the NTA official website and choose new registration. Enter your name, mobile number, and email address. Use details that will stay active for months.
  2. Fill in personal details. Add nationality, category, address, and parents' details. Your name must match your Class 10 certificate.
  3. Add academic details. Enter Class 10 and Class 12 information. Choose your exam city preferences and exam language.
  4. Upload documents. Add your photographs, signature, left thumb impression and certificates in the prescribed format.
  5. Pay the fee online. Select your category carefully, complete the payment, and save the receipt.
  6. Submit and download. Preview every field, submit the form, and keep printed copies of the confirmation page.

Use only the NTA official website for NEET UG 2027 Registration. Third-party links can mislead candidates and collect personal data.

What are the NEET UG application fees?

In 2026, the General fee was ₹1700. General EWS and OBC NCL candidates paid ₹1600. SC, ST, PwBD and Third Gender candidates paid ₹1000. NTA will confirm the 2027 fee in its bulletin.

Payment is online only. The fee is not refundable, so confirm your category before you pay.

Which documents are needed for the NEET UG form?

Every candidate needs two photographs, a scanned signature, and a left thumb impression. One photograph is passport size and the other is postcard size. Reserved category and PwBD candidates also need valid certificates. Exact file rules appear in the bulletin.

Which mistakes can delay or cancel your application?

Most problems come from mismatched names, wrong category claims, poor-quality images, and late payment. Check each entry against your Class 10 certificate and identity proof before final submission.

  1. Name mismatch. A spelling different from your Class 10 certificate can create trouble later.
  2. Wrong category. Select a reserved category only if you hold a valid certificate in the required format.
  3. Poor images. Blurry, shadowed, or computer-generated photographs may be rejected.
  4. Late payment. An unpaid form is incomplete, so pay well before the last date.
  5. Missing confirmation page. Download and print it, because it proves your submission.
  6. Unofficial websites. Fill the form only on the NTA official website.

NTA has generally allowed a correction window, but only some fields can be edited. Do not depend on it.

How can students prepare while waiting for the form?

Applying takes only a few hours, so keep your study routine steady. Prepare documents now. Spend the remaining time on NCERT revision in Biology, Physics, and Chemistry, plus regular practice tests.

Many students pair NCERT revision with NEET 2027 Online Preparation through a structured course. This keeps routine tasks like scanning documents from disturbing study hours. Keep a folder with scanned certificates and a signature sample. This saves time once the portal opens.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

FAQ 1: When will the NEET UG 2027 application form be released?

NTA has not announced the date. The 2026 form opened on 8 February, so February 2027 is possible. Rely only on the official bulletin for confirmed dates.

FAQ 2: Where should students fill the NEET UG application?

Candidates should use only the NTA official website for the form and all notices. Avoid unofficial links that ask for extra payment or personal details.

FAQ 3: Can students apply for NEET UG offline?

No. The application is submitted online only. Candidates do not need to send a printed copy to NTA. They should still keep the confirmation page safely.

FAQ 4: Is the NEET UG application fee refundable?

For 2026, the fee was not refundable in any category. Check the 2027 bulletin for the final rule, and confirm your category before paying.

FAQ 5: Can mistakes be corrected after submitting the form?

NTA has generally opened a correction window after the last date. Only certain fields can be edited, so it is better to verify every detail before submission.

FAQ 6: What should candidates carry to the exam centre after registration?

For 2026, candidates carried the admit card printout and a valid identity proof. They also carried a passport-size photograph and a postcard-size photograph for the proforma. Verify the 2027 list in the bulletin.
