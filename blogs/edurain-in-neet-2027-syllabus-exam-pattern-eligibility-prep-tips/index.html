NEET 2027 Syllabus, Exam Pattern, Eligibility & Prep Tips

If you are a student planning to pursue a career in medicine, starting your preparation early gives you a clear advantage. The NEET 2027 Syllabus covers subjects and topics from Class 11 and Class 12 Physics, Chemistry, and Biology as prescribed by NTA. This article is written for students in Class 10 or Class 11 who want to begin their NEET journey with a strong foundation. Here, you will learn about the complete syllabus, eligibility, exam pattern, preparation tips, useful resources, including online education platforms, and the career scope after qualifying NEET.


What Is NEET and Why Does It Matter?


NEET, or the National Eligibility cum Entrance Test, is the single national-level entrance exam for admission to MBBS, BDS, AYUSH, and other medical courses across India. It is conducted by the National Testing Agency (NTA) every year.


For students targeting 2027, understanding the syllabus early helps in:

  1. Building a strong conceptual base from Class 11 itself
  2. Avoiding last-minute stress and syllabus overload
  3. Planning a structured study schedule over two years
  4. Gaining confidence through consistent revision and practice


NEET scores are accepted by all government and private medical colleges in India, making it the most important exam for aspiring doctors.


What Does the NEET 2027 Syllabus Include?


The NEET 2027 Syllabus is based on the NCERT curriculum for Class 11 and Class 12. NTA has clearly defined the topics under three main subjects. Here is a structured breakdown.


Physics


Class 11 Topics:

  1. Physical World and Measurement
  2. Kinematics
  3. Laws of Motion
  4. Work, Energy, and Power
  5. Motion of System of Particles and Rigid Body
  6. Gravitation
  7. Properties of Bulk Matter
  8. Thermodynamics
  9. Behaviour of Perfect Gas and Kinetic Theory
  10. Oscillations and Waves


Class 12 Topics:

  1. Electrostatics
  2. Current Electricity
  3. Magnetic Effects of Current and Magnetism
  4. Electromagnetic Induction and Alternating Currents
  5. Electromagnetic Waves
  6. Optics
  7. Dual Nature of Matter and Radiation
  8. Atoms and Nuclei
  9. Electronic Devices


Chemistry


Class 11 Topics:

  1. Some Basic Concepts of Chemistry
  2. Structure of Atom
  3. Classification of Elements and Periodicity
  4. Chemical Bonding and Molecular Structure
  5. States of Matter
  6. Thermodynamics
  7. Equilibrium
  8. Redox Reactions
  9. Hydrogen
  10. Organic Chemistry: Some Basic Principles


Class 12 Topics:

  1. Solid State
  2. Solutions
  3. Electrochemistry
  4. Chemical Kinetics
  5. Surface Chemistry
  6. Coordination Compounds
  7. Aldehydes, Ketones, and Carboxylic Acids
  8. Amines
  9. Biomolecules
  10. Polymers and Chemistry in Everyday Life


Biology


Class 11 Topics:

  1. Diversity in the Living World
  2. Structural Organisation in Animals and Plants
  3. Cell: Structure and Function
  4. Plant Physiology
  5. Human Physiology


Class 12 Topics:

  1. Reproduction
  2. Genetics and Evolution
  3. Biology and Human Welfare
  4. Biotechnology and Its Applications
  5. Ecology and Environment


Biology carries the highest weightage in NEET with 360 marks out of 720, so students must give it maximum attention.


What Is the NEET 2027 Exam Pattern?


Understanding the exam pattern is just as important as knowing the syllabus. Here are the key details.


  1. Total Questions: 200 (students attempt 180)
  2. Total Marks: 720
  3. Sections: Physics, Chemistry, Botany, Zoology
  4. Each Section: 50 questions (35 mandatory + 15 optional, attempt any 10)
  5. Marking: +4 for correct, −1 for incorrect
  6. Duration: 3 hours 20 minutes
  7. Mode: Pen and paper (offline)
  8. Medium: English, Hindi, and several regional languages


Each section has two parts. Section A has 35 compulsory questions. Section B has 15 questions, out of which students need to answer any 10. This gives some flexibility during the exam.


Who Is Eligible to Appear for NEET 2027?


Before starting your preparation, make sure you meet the eligibility criteria set by NTA.


  1. Educational Qualification: Candidates must have passed or be appearing in Class 12 with Physics, Chemistry, Biology, and English.
  2. Minimum Marks: General category students need at least 50% marks in Class 12. OBC, SC, and ST students need 40%.
  3. Age Limit: Candidates must be at least 17 years old as of December 31, 2027. There is currently no upper age limit as per recent Supreme Court directives, but always check the latest NTA notification.
  4. Number of Attempts: As of now, there is no limit on the number of attempts.
  5. Nationality: Indian nationals, NRIs, OCIs, and foreign nationals can apply.


Always refer to the official NTA website for the most up-to-date eligibility details before filling the application form.


How Should You Start Preparing for NEET 2027?


Starting early is a major advantage, but preparation must be planned and consistent. Here are practical tips that work.


Build Your Foundation First

  1. Start with NCERT textbooks for all three subjects. NEET questions are directly or indirectly based on NCERT content.
  2. Read every line carefully, especially in Biology. Many questions test factual recall from NCERT.
  3. For Physics and Chemistry, understand derivations and practice numerical problems regularly.


Create a Realistic Study Plan

  1. Divide the syllabus into monthly and weekly targets.
  2. Give more time to Biology since it carries 50% of the total marks.
  3. Alternate between theory study and problem-solving to avoid burnout.


Use Quality Study Resources


Many students today benefit from online education platforms that offer video lectures, mock tests, and doubt-solving sessions. Platforms such as Edurain and others provide structured courses that follow the NTA syllabus closely.


  1. Watch video explanations for difficult topics in Physics and Chemistry.
  2. Join an online test series to practice under exam conditions.
  3. Use apps that offer daily quizzes and performance tracking.


Practice Previous Year Papers

  1. Solve at least 10 years of previous NEET papers.
  2. Analyze your mistakes and focus on weak areas.
  3. Time yourself to improve speed and accuracy.


Avoid These Common Mistakes

  1. Do not skip NCERT, thinking reference books are enough. NCERT is the primary source.
  2. Do not start with advanced books before finishing the basics.
  3. Do not ignore revision. Regular revision is more important than covering new topics repeatedly.
  4. Do not rely only on one study method. Combine reading, watching, writing, and practicing.



What Career Opportunities Open After Qualifying NEET?


NEET is not just for MBBS. Qualifying for this exam opens doors to multiple medical and healthcare careers.


  1. MBBS: The most popular choice, offered at government and private medical colleges.
  2. BDS: Bachelor of Dental Surgery for students interested in dentistry.
  3. AYUSH Courses: BAMS (Ayurveda), BHMS (Homeopathy), BUMS (Unani), BSMS (Siddha), and Naturopathy.
  4. BVSc: Bachelor of Veterinary Science and Animal Husbandry.
  5. BSc Nursing: Some nursing colleges accept NEET scores for admission.

What Is the Future Scope of Medical Careers in India?


India has a growing demand for healthcare professionals. The doctor-to-patient ratio in India is still below the WHO-recommended standard, which means there is significant scope for new doctors.


  1. Government hospitals and health centers are expanding across rural and urban areas.
  2. Private healthcare is one of the fastest-growing sectors in the country.
  3. Specializations in cardiology, neurology, orthopedics, and oncology offer excellent career growth.
  4. Research and teaching positions are available for those pursuing postgraduate and doctoral studies.


Many students also use online education platforms for preparing for postgraduate medical entrance exams such as NEET PG and INI CET after completing their MBBS.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)


1. Is the NEET 2027 syllabus different from previous years?

As of now, the NEET 2027 syllabus follows the same NCERT-based pattern. However, NTA may release updated notifications, so keep checking the official website for any changes.


2. How many months are needed to prepare for NEET properly?

Most successful candidates prepare for 18 to 24 months. Starting from Class 11 gives you enough time to cover the syllabus, revise, and practice mock tests.


3. What is the exam fee for NEET 2027?

NEET exam fees typically range from ₹1,600 to ₹1,700 for general category students. SC, ST, and PwD candidates pay a reduced fee. Exact amounts will be confirmed in the official notification.


4. Can I qualify NEET by studying only NCERT books?

NCERT forms the core of NEET preparation, especially for Biology. For Physics and Chemistry, you may need additional practice books for numerical and conceptual problems, but NCERT should always be your primary source.


5. What happens if I do not qualify NEET in the first attempt?

There is currently no limit on the number of attempts. You can appear for NEET again the following year with better preparation. Many successful candidates qualify after two or three attempts.


6. What is the expected salary after completing MBBS through NEET?

A government doctor after MBBS can expect a starting salary of approximately ₹60,000 to ₹1,00,000 per month. Salaries increase significantly after postgraduate specialization. Actual earnings depend on the institution, location, and experience.
