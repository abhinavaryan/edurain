Important Formulas and Identities to Remember

Every Basic Maths formula listed here supports faster calculation during full length JEE papers. Write these on a single revision sheet and review them before every practice session.

Algebra

  1. Quadratic roots: x = [-b ± √(b² - 4ac)] / 2a
  2. Sum of roots = -b/a, Product of roots = c/a
  3. (a + b)² = a² + 2ab + b², (a - b)² = a² - 2ab + b²

Trigonometry

  1. sin²θ + cos²θ = 1
  2. 1 + tan²θ = sec²θ, 1 + cot²θ = cosec²θ
  3. sin(A ± B) = sinA cosB ± cosA sinB

Logarithms

  1. log(mn) = log m + log n
  2. log(m/n) = log m - log n
  3. log(mⁿ) = n log m

Coordinate Geometry

  1. Distance formula: √[(x₂-x₁)² + (y₂-y₁)²]
  2. Slope of a line: m = (y₂-y₁)/(x₂-x₁)
  3. Equation of a line: y - y₁ = m(x - x₁)

These formulas repeat constantly across Physics numerical problems. Memorize them until recall becomes automatic during timed conditions.

Common Mistakes Students Make in Basic Maths

Sign errors in trigonometric identities cause the most frequent mistakes during JEE numerical questions. Students often forget quadrant rules when solving equations involving sinθ, cosθ, or tanθ.

Frequent errors include:

  1. Mixing up log base 10 and natural log during Chemistry calculations
  2. Forgetting the ± sign while solving quadratic equations
  3. Applying wrong angle addition formulas under time pressure
  4. Skipping domain restrictions while solving functions
  5. Misreading slope direction in coordinate geometry graphs

Most errors happen due to rushed practice rather than lack of knowledge. Slow, deliberate solving during early preparation prevents these habits from forming.
