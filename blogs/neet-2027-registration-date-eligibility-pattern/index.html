NEET 2027 Registration Date: When Will Applications Open? | EduRain Blog

NTA has not released the official NEET UG 2027 notification or information bulletin as of September 2026. So the NEET 2027 Registration Date is still unconfirmed. Past cycles point to a likely window, and you can prepare for it now. This guide covers important dates, eligibility, exam pattern, syllabus, and application steps.

NEET UG 2027: Important Dates

NTA has announced no official dates for NEET UG 2027. Applications are expected between December and February, and the exam is tentatively set for early May 2027. Treat every date as an estimate until the bulletin arrives.

NTA will publish the registration schedule in the information bulletin PDF. Coaching sites and news portals can only estimate. One source expects the form in the second week of February 2027. Another says registration usually opens in December or January.

Who Can Apply for NEET UG 2027?

Candidates who complete 17 years of age by 31 December 2027 and pass Class 12 with Physics, Chemistry, Biology, and English can apply. Final rules will appear in the 2027 bulletin. Use the 2026 rules as a guide.

  1. Age: The minimum age is 17 years by 31 December 2027. There is currently no upper age limit, subject to the latest NTA notification.
  2. Subjects: Class 12 with Physics, Chemistry, and Biology or Biotechnology, plus English.
  3. Marks: Previous bulletins asked for 50 percent for the general category. The requirement was lower for reserved categories.
  4. Attempts: No attempt limit is confirmed at present.
  5. Appearing students: Class 12 students awaiting results can usually apply.

Verify every point in the official bulletin. Proposed changes to age and attempts are under discussion, but none is officially implemented.

NEET UG 2027 Exam Pattern

The latest official pattern had 180 compulsory multiple choice questions for 720 marks. NTA has not confirmed the 2027 pattern. A computer based test was proposed, but the Centre had not taken a final decision.

Each correct answer earned four marks. Each wrong answer cost one mark. Unattempted questions carried no penalty.

How to Apply for NEET UG 2027: Documents and Steps

The application is filled online only on the official NTA portal. Keep your documents ready before the window opens. Complete all seven steps in one sitting.

Documents to keep ready:

  1. Class 10 certificate for name and date of birth
  2. Class 12 marksheet or admit card
  3. Aadhaar card
  4. Recent passport size photograph
  5. Clear scan of your signature
  6. Category or PwBD certificate, if applicable
  7. Active email address and mobile number

For NEET UG 2026, candidates also uploaded a live photograph and chose three exam cities. Prepare for both.

Steps to apply:

  1. Open the official NTA NEET website and select new registration.
  2. Enter your name and date of birth as on your Class 10 certificate.
  3. Verify your mobile number and email address.
  4. Fill in your academic, category, and address details.
  5. Upload your photograph, signature, and other documents.
  6. Choose your preferred exam cities.
  7. Pay the fee online and save the confirmation page.

The fee is not refundable once paid. Review every field before payment.

Common Mistakes Students Make During NEET Registration

The most common errors are mismatched details, wrong file specifications, missed corrections, and unofficial websites. Each can delay or cancel your application. Check every field before you pay.

  1. Entering a name or birth date that differs from the Class 10 certificate.
  2. Uploading a blurred or oversized photograph or signature.
  3. Choosing exam cities without checking travel distance.
  4. Paying fees or sharing details on unofficial websites.
  5. Skipping the correction window after submission.
  6. Treating the expected NEET 2027 Registration Date as final and missing the real notice.
  7. Waiting for the last day, when the portal often slows down.
