Who Can Apply for JEE Main 2027?

Students who have passed Class 12 or are appearing in it can apply. NTA does not set an age limit for JEE Main. Eligibility details appear in the information bulletin.

Keep these points in mind:

  1. Paper 1 is for B.E. and B.Tech admissions.
  2. Paper 2 is for B.Arch and B.Planning admissions.
  3. You can appear in one session or both.
  4. Only one application is accepted per candidate.

Documents and Details to Keep Ready

Keep your documents ready before the link goes live. Missing or mismatched details cause most delays. Your Class 10 certificate is the main reference for your name and date of birth.

Prepare the following:

  1. Class 10 certificate or marksheet for name and date of birth
  2. Aadhaar details, updated and matching your records
  3. Scanned passport-size photograph
  4. Scanned signature
  5. Category or PwD certificate, if applicable
  6. Active mobile number and email address
  7. Bank or card details for the fee payment

NTA advised candidates in the last cycle to update Aadhaar details before applying. Check the photo and signature size limits in the bulletin. A live photograph may also be requested.

How to Apply for JEE Main 2027 Step by Step

The application has four parts. These are registration, form filling, document upload, and fee payment. Use only the official NTA portal for all of them.

  1. Open jeemain.nta.nic.in and click the JEE Main 2027 registration link.
  2. Read the information bulletin fully.
  3. Create a new registration with your name, mobile number, and email.
  4. Save the application number and password that you receive.
  5. Log in and fill in personal, academic, and address details.
  6. Choose your paper, exam cities, and medium of exam.
  7. Upload the photograph, signature, and certificates in the required format.
  8. Pay the fee online and download the confirmation page.

Session 1 and Session 2 have separate application windows. You must apply again for Session 2.

How to Prepare While You Wait for Registration

Start syllabus work now, because the January exam is close. Registration takes little time. Preparation is what needs months. Follow the NTA syllabus based on the rationalized NCERT content. Do not spend time on deleted chapters. Use Class 11 and Class 12 NCERT books as your base.

Physics needs strong basics in mechanics, electrostatics, and modern physics. Chemistry rewards NCERT reading in inorganic topics and regular practice in physical numericals. Maths depends on calculus, algebra, and coordinate geometry.

A structured course can help if self-study feels scattered. Choose online coaching for JEE Main 2027 only after checking the syllabus coverage, test series, and doubt support. Trial classes help you judge the teaching style.

Common Mistakes During JEE Main 2027 Registration

Most registration errors come from rushing. A wrong detail can block verification later. Avoid these specific mistakes:

  1. Name or date of birth mismatch: Enter details exactly as in the Class 10 certificate.
  2. Wrong category selection: Upload a valid certificate for the category you choose.
  3. Incorrect photo or signature size: Check file size and format before uploading.
  4. Skipping the Aadhaar update: Verification issues can arise when records do not match.
  5. Applying more than once: NTA does not accept multiple applications.
  6. Using unofficial websites: Pay fees only on the NTA portal.
  7. Waiting for the last date: Server load rises near the deadline.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Has NTA announced the JEE Main 2027 registration date?

No. As of late September 2026, the exact date is not official. Expect it in the fourth week of October 2026, and confirm it on the NTA portal.

2. What is the expected last date for Session 1 registration?

The window usually stays open for about a month. The last date is expected around the fourth week of November 2026. NTA will confirm it in the bulletin.

3. Is there an age limit for JEE Main 2027?

No. NTA has not set an age limit for JEE Main. You must meet the Class 12 qualifying criteria in the information bulletin.

4. Can I appear in both sessions of JEE Main 2027?

Yes. Candidates can appear in one session or both. Each session has its own application window, so you must apply separately.

5. Which website should I use to apply?

Use only jeemain.nta.nic.in, the official NTA portal. Avoid third-party links, and never pay the fee through unofficial pages.
