Class 10 Maths Most Important Questions Chapter-wise for CBSE Board Exam | EduRain Blog

The most important questions in CBSE Class 10 Maths come from Algebra, Geometry, Trigonometry, Mensuration, and Statistics, because these units carry most of the 80 theory marks. This guide lists the most important Class 10 Maths questions chapter-wise, with formulas, worked answers, and revision steps for the 2027 board exam.

The details below follow the CBSE syllabus for the 2026 to 27 session. Always confirm final exam dates on cbse.gov.in.

What Is the CBSE Class 10 Maths Exam Pattern for 2027?

The theory paper carries 80 marks, and Internal Assessment carries 20 marks. The paper has 38 questions across five sections, A to E. Students should confirm whether they are studying Standard (code 041) or Basic (code 241).

Section A has 20 one-mark questions. Section B has five questions of two marks, and Section C has six questions of three marks. Section D has four questions of five marks, and Section E has three case-based questions of four marks each.

For 2027, CBSE follows a two-exam system for Class 10. Phase 1 is mandatory and is expected in February to March. Phase 2 is expected in May and gives students a chance to improve their scores.

Which Number Systems and Algebra Questions Are Most Important?

Real Numbers carries 6 marks, and Algebra carries 20 marks. Prioritise irrationality proofs, HCF and LCM, zeroes of polynomials, linear equations, discriminant problems, and arithmetic progression sums, as these are standard question types in the board paper.

Remember these formulas: sum of zeroes = −b/a, product of zeroes = c/a, discriminant D = b² − 4ac, and Sₙ = n/2 [2a + (n − 1)d]. Also practise the step-by-step proofs that √2, √3, and √5 are irrational.

Which Geometry and Coordinate Geometry Questions Should Students Practise?

Geometry carries 15 marks, and Coordinate Geometry carries 6 marks. Focus on similar triangle theorems, tangent properties, the distance formula, and the section formula. Write every reason clearly, because board answers are marked step by step.

Which Trigonometry, Mensuration, Statistics and Probability Questions Matter Most?

Trigonometry carries 12 marks, Mensuration 10 marks, and Statistics and Probability 11 marks. Practise identities, heights and distances, sector area, solids and cones, mean, median, and mode of grouped data, and basic probability problems.

What Common Mistakes Reduce Marks in Class 10 Maths?

Most lost marks come from sign errors, missing units, skipped steps, and unclear reasons in proofs. Careful presentation matters because three-mark and five-mark answers are awarded marks for each correct step.

  1. Quadratic Equations: Students forget the sign of b or reject valid roots without checking.
  2. Trigonometry: Students forget to state that the angle is acute or mix up complementary angle identities.
  3. Mensuration: Students mix units or use a different value of π from the one given in the question.
  4. Statistics: Students choose the wrong median class or miscalculate cumulative frequency.
  5. Geometry: Students skip the given and to prove statements and do not write reasons.

How Can Students Revise Class 10 Maths Important Questions Step by Step?

Start with NCERT examples, then solve exercise questions, practise chapter-wise important questions, and finish with timed CBSE sample papers. Record every mistake in a notebook and revisit it before the next test.

  1. Read the NCERT chapter summary and solve all solved examples.
  2. Complete the NCERT exercises, then attempt Class 10 Maths important questions for the board exam from each chapter.
  3. Prepare a one-page formula sheet for each unit and revise it daily.
  4. Solve CBSE sample papers in a three-hour sitting. CBSE publishes these at cbseacademic.nic.in.
  5. Review errors, mark weak chapters, and repeat them the next week.

Algebra carries the most marks at 20 out of 80. It includes Polynomials, Pair of Linear Equations in Two Variables, Quadratic Equations, and Arithmetic Progressions.

The 2026 to 27 syllabus has 14 chapters across seven units. They range from Real Numbers to Probability.
