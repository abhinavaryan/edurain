CBSE Class 12 Physics: Most Important Questions for Board Exam 2027 | EduRain Blog

The most important questions for the 2027 board exam come from core units such as Electrostatics, Magnetic Effects of Current and Magnetism, Optics, and Atoms and Nuclei. This guide covers likely question types, key derivations, numericals, and a revision order, so CBSE Class 12 Physics: Most Important Questions can be practised in a clear sequence.

What are the most important questions for CBSE Class 12 Physics 2027?

Important questions are those that keep appearing in NCERT exercises, sample papers, and past board papers. They include derivations, ray diagrams, numericals, and case-based items. Practise them chapter-wise and understand the logic behind each answer.

The theory paper is 70 marks for 3 hours. Recent papers followed the pattern below. Confirm it against the 2026 to 2027 sample paper on the official CBSE academic website.

Expect these question types:

  1. Derivations carrying 3 or 5 marks
  2. Ray diagrams with formula-based reasoning
  3. Numericals using NCERT formulas
  4. Conceptual MCQs and assertion-reason items
  5. Case-based questions on circuits, optics, and modern physics

Which chapters are most important for Class 12 Physics board exams?

Chapters from units with higher marks deserve more practice time. Among the unit weightages published for 2026 to 2027, Electrostatics carries 16 marks and Magnetic Effects of Current and Magnetism carries 17 marks. Electronic Devices carries 7 marks.

What are the most repeated questions in Class 12 Physics?

Repeated questions are patterns, not guarantees. Past papers show that some concepts return often in different forms. Treat the list below as a revision priority, not a prediction.

  1. Gauss's law and its applications
  2. Wheatstone bridge and potentiometer
  3. Biot-Savart law and Ampere's law
  4. Lenz's law and induced emf
  5. Transformer working and losses
  6. Lens maker's formula and prism refraction
  7. Young's double-slit experiment
  8. Photoelectric effect graphs
  9. Bohr model and energy level transitions
  10. Binding energy curve
  11. Diode as a rectifier

How to prepare for CBSE Class 12 Physics board exam 2027?

Start with NCERT, move to sample papers, and then practise timed papers. Revise derivations and formulas every week. Keep an error notebook for mistakes in numericals and diagrams.

  1. Read each NCERT chapter and solve all in-text examples.
  2. Solve NCERT exercise questions before any reference book.
  3. Write derivations on paper with neat diagrams.
  4. Practise CBSE Class 12 Physics: Most Important Questions under a 3-hour timer.
  5. Review mistakes and redo them after two days.
  6. Solve the latest CBSE sample paper and previous years' papers.

Common mistakes to avoid:

  1. Forgetting units or sign conventions in optics
  2. Skipping ray diagrams or labels
  3. Memorising results without the derivation steps
  4. Ignoring case-based questions until the last week

Which topics should students revise first in Class 12 Physics?

Revise topics that combine high marks with steady scoring potential. Begin with Electrostatics, Current Electricity, and Magnetic Effects. Then move to Optics, Modern Physics, and Semiconductor Electronics. Finish with Alternating Current and Electromagnetic Waves.

Use this order:

  1. Electrostatics and Current Electricity
  2. Moving Charges, Magnetism, and Induction
  3. Ray Optics and Wave Optics
  4. Dual Nature, Atoms, and Nuclei
  5. Semiconductor Electronics
  6. Alternating Current and Electromagnetic Waves

Key Takeaways

  1. The theory paper is 70 marks, and the practical is 30 marks.
  2. Electrostatics carries 16 marks and Magnetic Effects carries 17 marks in the 2026 to 2027 unit weightage.
  3. Derivations, ray diagrams, and numericals form the core of board preparation.
  4. NCERT-based important questions should come before reference books.
  5. Timed sample papers and an error notebook improve accuracy.

Conclusion and Next Steps

Strong Physics preparation depends on NCERT clarity, regular derivation practice, and accurate numericals. Start with the high-mark units, solve questions chapter-wise, and test yourself with timed papers. Next, pick one chapter today, write its derivations, and solve its NCERT numericals without help.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

FAQ 1: How many marks does Class 12 Physics carry in the CBSE board exam?

Physics carries 70 marks for theory and 30 marks for practical, making 100 in total. The theory paper is 3 hours long.

FAQ 2: Are NCERT questions enough for board exam preparation?

NCERT is the base for the syllabus and should be completed first. Add sample papers and previous year papers to practise different question styles and time management.

FAQ 3: Which numerical topics should students practise most?

Practise lens formula, circuits with internal resistance, photoelectric effect, LCR resonance, and nuclear binding energy. These topics use clear formulas and appear in many question forms.

FAQ 4: How should students practise derivations?

Write each derivation by hand with a labelled diagram. Learn the starting law, the main steps, and the final expression. Revise them weekly instead of memorising them the night before.

FAQ 5: How should sample papers be used for revision?

Solve them in 3 hours, check answers against the marking scheme, and note every mistake. Redo weak questions after two days to confirm the concept is clear.

FAQ 6: Do case-based questions need separate preparation?

Yes. They test application of concepts in a short passage. Practise them after finishing each chapter, and read the passage carefully before answering.
