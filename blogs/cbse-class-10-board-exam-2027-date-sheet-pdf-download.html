CBSE Class 10 Board Exam 2027 Date Sheet: When Will Exams Start? Full Schedule | EduRain Blog

The CBSE Class 10 Board Exam 2027 Date Sheet was released on November 6, 2026. Phase 1 theory exams begin on February 16, 2027, with Painting, and reports place the last paper on March 9, 2027. Mathematics is on February 17, and Science is on February 22. The optional Phase 2 exam is planned for May 2027.

Class 10 students now have a clear timetable for the 2026 to 2027 session. This guide explains the start date, the subject-wise schedule, exam timings, Phase 2, and the steps to download the official PDF. It also shows how to plan revision around the gaps between papers.

When Do CBSE Class 10 Board Exams Start in 2027?

CBSE Class 10 Phase 1 exams start on February 16, 2027, and reports on the released schedule say they end on March 9, 2027. Every paper begins at 10:30 AM. Most papers end at 1:30 PM.

From today, November 7, 2026, the first paper is about 14 weeks away. That is enough time for two full revision cycles if students start now.

Class 10 now follows a two-phase system. Phase 1 in February and March is compulsory. Phase 2 in May is optional and meant for improvement.

Practical and internal assessment work happens before the theory papers. Reports based on the usual calendar say practicals for most schools run from January to mid-February 2027, while winter-bound schools finish theirs earlier. Treat these as expected windows and follow the dates your school gives you.

How Can Students Plan Study Time Around These Exam Dates?

Count backwards from each paper. Use the three-to-four-day gap between core subjects for revision and timed practice, and begin the first full revision of Mathematics and Science now, since they come first.

Start by writing every paper date on a wall calendar. Mark Mathematics on February 17, Science on February 22, English on February 26, Hindi on March 3, and Social Science on March 8. Then split the weeks left before February into subject blocks.

With only about 14 weeks left, a fixed weekly routine matters. Joining the Best CBSE Class 10 Online Coaching programme gives you chapter-wise live classes, regular doubt-solving, and mock tests mapped to the new date sheet, so every week has a clear target. Pick a course that follows the latest CBSE syllabus and still leaves you time to revise on your own.

Keep one evening each week for a full-length paper under exam conditions. Match the timing of the real exam, which starts at 10:30 AM, so your body clock is ready on the day.

What Step-by-Step Preparation Strategy Fits the Time Left?

A workable plan has five parts: finish the syllabus by December, revise NCERT books, solve CBSE sample papers, practise in timed sessions, and review mistakes every week. Follow these steps in order.

  1. Download the date sheet PDF and note each paper, its code, and its timing.
  2. Complete any pending chapters by December, using the syllabus for 2026 to 2027 on the CBSE academic website.
  3. Revise NCERT textbooks fully, because most board questions rely on them.
  4. Solve the sample papers CBSE has published for Class 10 and study the marking scheme.
  5. Attempt a timed paper every week and keep a notebook of repeated errors.

For guided revision, the Edurain Online Education Platform can be used as an additional learning resource alongside NCERT books and school notes. Pick one main resource and avoid switching often.

When Is the Class 10 Phase 2 Exam in 2027?

The Class 10 Phase 2 exam is planned for May 2027. It is optional, and students can use it to improve their scores in up to three subjects. Phase 1 remains compulsory for everyone.

Reports on the released schedule list the Phase 2 papers in May 2027 without exact dates. Wait for the official notice before fixing a revision plan around specific May dates.

Reports also describe the system as the best of two, meaning the better result counts. Confirm the exact rule in the latest CBSE circular before deciding whether to sit Phase 2.

How Can Students Download the Date Sheet PDF From the Official Website?

Open the official website of CBSE, click the Main Website option, find the Class 10 date sheet 2027 link under the Latest @ CBSE section, and download the PDF. Save a copy on your phone and print one for your study table.

Students can also check the academic section of the official CBSE website for circulars and syllabus documents. Use only official sources for final dates.

Social media screenshots and unofficial timetable posts often contain errors. If a date in any article, including this one, differs from the official PDF, trust the PDF.

Which Common Mistakes Should Students Avoid With the Timetable?

The most common mistakes are trusting unofficial screenshots, ignoring paper timings, mixing up subject codes, and spending too long on one subject. Avoiding these keeps your revision accurate and your exam day calm.

  1. Do not assume every paper ends at 1:30 PM. Painting, Tourism, Physical Activity Trainer, and Computing papers end at 12:30 PM.
  2. Do not choose Mathematics Standard or Basic casually. Your school registration decides your paper.
  3. Do not leave Social Science for the last week, even though it is the final core paper on March 8.
  4. Do not skip the gap days. Use them for revision and not for new chapters.
  5. Do not ignore school notices about the admit card, centre, and reporting time.

Key Takeaways

  1. The Class 10 date sheet for 2027 was released on November 6, 2026.
  2. Phase 1 starts on February 16, 2027, and reports place the last paper on March 9, 2027.
  3. Mathematics is on February 17, Science on February 22, English on February 26, Hindi on March 3, and Social Science on March 8.
  4. Most papers run from 10:30 AM to 1:30 PM, while some shorter papers end at 12:30 PM.
  5. Phase 2 is optional, planned for May 2027, and allows improvement in up to three subjects.
  6. Always confirm final dates in the official PDF on the official website of CBSE.

Conclusion and Next Steps

The CBSE Class 10 Board Exam 2027 Date Sheet gives students about 14 weeks before the first core paper. Use that time to finish the syllabus, revise NCERT, and practise with sample papers. Your next step is to download the official PDF today and mark every paper date on a calendar.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

FAQ 1: When will the CBSE 10th timetable 2027 come out, and when do the exams begin?

The timetable has already been released on November 6, 2026. Phase 1 begins on February 16, 2027, so students asking when the board exams start can begin revision plans right away.

FAQ 2: What is the exam timing for Class 10 papers in 2027?

Every paper starts at 10:30 AM. Most papers end at 1:30 PM, while shorter papers such as Painting, Introduction to Tourism, and Physical Activity Trainer end at 12:30 PM.

FAQ 3: Which dates are fixed for Maths, Science, English, Hindi and Social Science?

Mathematics is on February 17, Science on February 22, English on February 26, Hindi on March 3, and Social Science on March 8, 2027, as listed in reports on the released schedule.

FAQ 4: Are the released dates final?

Reports describe the schedule as released, and some call the dates tentative. Final confirmation comes only from the official CBSE notice, so check the official website of CBSE and your school circulars for any revision.

FAQ 5: Is the Phase 2 exam compulsory for Class 10 students?

No. Phase 1 is compulsory, while Phase 2 in May 2027 is optional and meant for improving marks in up to three subjects.

FAQ 6: Where can students download the Class 10 date sheet PDF?

Visit the official website of CBSE, open the Main Website, and select the Class 10 date sheet 2027 link under Latest @ CBSE. Download and save the PDF for reference.
