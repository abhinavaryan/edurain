NEET UG 2027 Syllabus: Subject-Wise Chapters | EduRain Blog

The NEET UG 2027 Syllabus covers Physics, Chemistry, and Biology from NCERT Class 11 and Class 12. Knowing the exact chapters helps you plan study time and avoid removed topics. This guide lists the chapters subject-wise. It also explains the current release status, marks distribution, past trend chapters, common mistakes, and a study plan you can follow from today.

Current Status and Marks Distribution

NMC and NTA had not released a separate syllabus for 2027 by mid-September 2026. Follow the latest officially prescribed syllabus. Check NTA and NMC for any changes.

Some sources report 79 chapters after NCERT rationalisation. Treat that count as unconfirmed. The official document decides the final list.

Removed portions need no study time. Compare any third-party list with nmc.org.in and neet.nta.nic.in.

Biology carries the most marks. Recent papers had 180 compulsory questions worth 720 marks.

SubjectQuestionsMarks
Physics45180
Chemistry45180
Biology (Botany and Zoology)90360
Total180720


Confirm this pattern in the 2027 information bulletin. NTA may change the format or mode.

Physics Chapters

Physics has units from both Class 11 and Class 12. Mechanics and electricity form the largest groups. Study each unit with formulas and numericals together.

ClassUnits
Class 11Units and Measurement, Kinematics, Laws of Motion, Work, Energy and Power, Rotational Motion, Gravitation, Properties of Solids and Liquids, Thermodynamics, Kinetic Theory of Gases, Oscillations and Waves
Class 12Electrostatics, Current Electricity, Magnetic Effects of Current and Magnetism, Electromagnetic Induction and Alternating Currents, Electromagnetic Waves, Optics, Dual Nature of Matter and Radiation, Atoms and Nuclei, Electronic Devices


Check the official list for any topic marked as removed.

Chemistry Chapters

Chemistry splits into Physical, Inorganic, and Organic. Each branch needs a different method. Physical needs numericals, Inorganic needs recall, and Organic needs reaction logic.

BranchKey Chapters
PhysicalBasic Concepts, Structure of Atom, Thermodynamics, Equilibrium, Redox Reactions, Solutions, Electrochemistry, Chemical Kinetics
InorganicPeriodic Classification, Chemical Bonding, p-Block Elements, d and f-Block Elements, Coordination Compounds
OrganicGeneral Organic Chemistry, Hydrocarbons, Haloalkanes and Haloarenes, Alcohols, Phenols and Ethers, Aldehydes, Ketones and Acids, Amines, Biomolecules


Read Inorganic Chemistry directly from NCERT lines. Questions often come from statements, tables, and exceptions.

Biology Chapters

Biology has 90 questions across Botany and Zoology. Almost every question traces back to NCERT text. Diagrams, tables, and boxed content also matter.

ClassUnits and Chapters
Class 11Diversity in the Living World, Structural Organisation in Plants and Animals, Cell Structure and Function, Plant Physiology, Human Physiology
Class 12Reproduction, Genetics and Evolution, Biology and Human Welfare, Biotechnology, Ecology and Environment


Human Physiology and Genetics usually deserve extra time. Revise them every week.

High Weightage Chapters Based on Past Trends

Past papers show repeated questions from Human Physiology, Genetics, Chemical Bonding, Organic Chemistry, and Modern Physics. These are trends only. They do not predict the 2027 paper.

Common Mistakes Students Make with This Syllabus

The most common mistake is studying from old notes. Those notes may include removed chapters. This wastes weeks that Biology and Physics need.

Watch for these specific mistakes:

  1. Skipping Class 11 chapters while focusing on Class 12.
  2. Ignoring NCERT figures, tables, and footnotes in Biology.
  3. Memorising Organic reactions without learning the mechanism.
  4. Dropping chapters because a trend table ranks them lower.
  5. Starting NEET 2027 Online Preparation without a chapter checklist.
  6. Waiting for the official release before starting any chapter.

Step-by-Step Plan to Cover the Syllabus

Cover the syllabus in three rounds: first reading, practice, and revision. Break each subject into chapters and tick them off. A fixed daily slot for each subject keeps progress steady.

  1. Download the latest syllabus. Mark each chapter against the NCERT index.
  2. Divide chapters by class. Finish Class 11 before going deep into Class 12.
  3. Read NCERT line by line. Do this first for Biology and Chemistry.
  4. Solve chapter-wise questions. Do this for every Physics unit.
  5. Add weekly tests. Review each wrong answer and note the reason.
  6. Revise in the last phase. Use short notes, formula sheets, and diagram lists.

Students who prefer a guided routine can follow NEET 2027 Online Preparation with live classes and doubt support. Choose a course that follows NCERT order. Make sure it matches the latest syllabus.

Key Takeaways

  1. Follow the latest officially prescribed syllabus until NMC or NTA issues a 2027 notice.
  2. Treat any chapter count as unconfirmed until the official document appears.
  3. Biology carries 360 of 720 marks, so give it steady daily time.
  4. Past trend tables guide study order, not chapter selection.
  5. Read NCERT Biology and Inorganic Chemistry line by line.
  6. Practice Physics numericals after every chapter.

Conclusion & Next Steps

Mastering the syllabus begins with a clear chapter list. Open the latest NMC document today. Mark each chapter as not started, in progress, or done. Update the list every week. Frequently Asked Questions

1. Has the syllabus for NEET UG 2027 been released?

Not by mid-September 2026. Follow the latest officially prescribed syllabus. Recheck NTA and NMC when a notice appears.

2. How many chapters does the syllabus have?

Some sources report 79 chapters, but the count is unconfirmed. Check the official NMC document for the final list.

3. Which subject has the highest marks?

Biology has 90 questions worth 360 marks. Physics and Chemistry each carry 180 marks. Confirm the pattern in the 2027 bulletin.

4. Which chapters appeared most often in past papers?

Human Physiology, Genetics, Chemical Bonding, Organic Chemistry, and Modern Physics appeared often. These are trends, not predictions. Cover the full syllabus.

5. Do I need to study deleted chapters?

No. Skip portions that NMC has removed after rationalisation. Compare your notes with the official list before studying any chapter.

6. Which Biology units need the most study time?

Human Physiology and Genetics and Evolution usually deserve extra time. Both are broad and rich in NCERT detail. Revise them every week along with diagrams and tables.
