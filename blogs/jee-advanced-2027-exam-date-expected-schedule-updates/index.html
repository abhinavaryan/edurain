JEE Advanced 2027 Exam Date: Complete Schedule, Registration and Exam Pattern | EduRain Blog

The JEE Advanced 2027 Exam Date has not been officially announced yet. Based on past cycles, the exam is expected in May 2027, most likely in the middle of the month. IIT Delhi is expected to organise it. This guide covers the expected dates, registration, eligibility, pattern, application steps, and preparation.

When Is the Expected Date and Time of JEE Advanced 2027?

The exam is expected in May 2027, probably in the second or third week. Paper 1 usually runs from 9:00 AM to 12:00 PM and Paper 2 from 2:30 PM to 5:30 PM. The official notice will confirm both.

Treat these as planning estimates, not final dates. The Joint Admission Board will fix the schedule and publish it on jeeadv.ac.in. Some education portals expect the announcement around December 2026, but that is not official.

The exam is organised by seven zonal IITs on a rotation. IIT Roorkee conducted the exam on 17 May 2026. IIT Delhi is next in the rotation, but the official confirmation is still awaited. The final JEE Advanced 2027 Exam Date should therefore be checked only on the official website.

When Will JEE Advanced 2027 Registration Start?

Registration is expected to begin in April 2027, after JEE Main Session 2 results are declared. The window is usually short. Keep your documents ready and follow jeeadv.ac.in for confirmed dates.

Registration happens online. Candidates need their JEE Main details and must pay the fee before the last date, which is expected in May 2027. Foreign candidates usually register in a separate window around April.

Fee amounts depend on category and gender. They are listed in the information brochure, so do not rely on unofficial figures.

What Is the Complete JEE Advanced 2027 Timetable?

The full timetable will be released by the organising IIT. Based on past patterns, registration comes in April, the admit card and exam in May, and the result around the start of June. All of these are expected, not confirmed.

Who Is Eligible and What Is the Cutoff for JEE Advanced 2027?

Candidates must be among the top 2,50,000 JEE Main qualifiers for the B.E. and B.Tech paper. They must also meet the age, attempt, and Class 12 rules. Cutoff marks are announced with the result and change every year.

The main points based on the current rules are:

  1. A candidate can attempt the exam a maximum of two times in two consecutive years.
  2. The Class 12 appearing or passing year must match the brochure rules.
  3. The age limit and the rules on earlier IIT admission are given in the brochure.
  4. Category-wise relaxations apply as stated officially.

Cutoffs are of two kinds. One is the minimum marks in each subject. The other is the minimum aggregate marks. Both are category-wise and are published with the result. No fixed number exists before that, so avoid any website that claims one.

What Is the JEE Advanced 2027 Exam Pattern and Marking Scheme?

The exam has two compulsory papers of three hours each. Both cover Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics. Recent years used a computer-based test. The question types and marking scheme change every year and are published in the brochure.

The marking scheme is the part that students often assume. Do not copy last year's marks per question. Read the instructions on the 2027 brochure and the official mock test. The organising IIT usually activates a mock test link on the website. Attempting it helps with time management and the interface.

How to Apply for JEE Advanced 2027?

Apply online on jeeadv.ac.in after qualifying in JEE Main and when the registration window opens. Fill in the form, upload documents, pay the fee, and save the confirmation. Follow the steps below.

  1. Confirm that you are in the eligible JEE Main rank list.
  2. Visit jeeadv.ac.in and open the registration link.
  3. Log in with your JEE Main details.
  4. Enter your personal and academic information carefully.
  5. Upload the photograph, signature, and certificates asked for, such as category or PwD documents.
  6. Choose the exam city as instructed.
  7. Pay the fee online before the last date.
  8. Download and keep a copy of the confirmation page.

Check every detail before submitting. Name, category, and date of birth errors can cause problems at the admit card and counselling stages.

What Is the JEE Advanced 2027 Syllabus and Preparation Strategy?

The syllabus covers Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics at the Class 11 and 12 level. The official syllabus is on jeeadv.ac.in. Prepare with concept clarity first, then timed practice on higher-difficulty problems.

Follow this order of preparation:

  1. Download the official syllabus and mark each topic as strong, average or weak.
  2. Finish NCERT concepts before moving to advanced problem sets.
  3. Solve questions that combine two or more concepts, which are common in this exam.
  4. Take timed full-length tests every week and review each mistake.
  5. Maintain an error log for formulas, calculation slips, and misread questions.
  6. Revise short notes and previous year papers in the final weeks.

Common mistakes include skipping Class 11 topics, ignoring Chemistry, and memorising formulas without derivations. Practise numerical answer questions regularly, since they have no options to guess from. Do not treat any estimated weightage as official unless the IIT publishes it.

  4. Only the top 2,50,000 JEE Main qualifiers can register.
  5. Both papers are compulsory, and each lasts three hours.
  6. Marking schemes and cutoffs change yearly, so rely on the official brochure.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

FAQ 1: Has the official exam schedule been announced?

No. The schedule has not been officially announced yet. May 2027 is only an expected month based on earlier cycles. Wait for the notice on jeeadv.ac.in.

FAQ 2: Which IIT will organise the exam in 2027?

IIT Delhi is expected to organise it under the rotation. IIT Roorkee organised the 2026 exam. Official confirmation is still pending from the Joint Admission Board.

FAQ 3: How many attempts are allowed?

A candidate can attempt the exam a maximum of two times in two consecutive years. Always confirm this against the 2027 information brochure.

FAQ 4: How long is each paper?

Each paper lasts three hours. Paper 1 and Paper 2 are both compulsory, and both include Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics.

FAQ 5: Is the cutoff fixed before the exam?

No. Cutoff marks are declared with the result. They depend on the difficulty of the paper and the performance of candidates in each category.

FAQ 6: When will the admit card be released?

The admit card is expected in May 2027, before the exam. The organising IIT will confirm the exact date. Download it only from the official website.
