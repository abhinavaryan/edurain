CBSE Class 10 Board Exam 2027: Paper Pattern, Marking Scheme and Question Types | EduRain Blog

The CBSE Class 10 Board Exam 2027: Paper Pattern, Marking Scheme keeps each main subject at 100 marks: 80 for the three-hour theory paper and 20 for the school internal assessment. About half the theory marks go to competency-based questions, 20 percent to objective questions, and 30 percent to written answers. Phase 1 begins on February 16, 2027.

This guide explains the paper structure, key dates, question types, and subject-wise patterns for Class 10 students. It keeps official dates separate from pattern details reported by education sources.

What Is the Overall Structure of the CBSE Class 10 Exam Pattern 2027?

Each main subject carries 100 marks, made up of an 80-mark written paper and 20 marks of internal assessment. Class 10 now has two board exam phases, and Phase 1 is compulsory for every student.

CBSE released the 2027 date sheet on November 6, 2026. Students who want to improve their marks can take Phase 2 in up to three subjects. The key dates are below.

EventDetail

Paper timing10:30 am to 1:30 pm for most papers, and some end at 12:30 pm

Admit cardsExpected in January 2027

For comparison, Phase 2 in 2026 was held from May 15 to 21. That is a past trend, not a confirmed 2027 schedule.

The 20 internal marks come from the school. In Science, they are divided into periodic assessment (5 plus 5 marks), subject enrichment work (5 marks), and a portfolio (5 marks).

How Does the CBSE Class 10 Marking Scheme 2027 Divide Marks?

Theory marks follow a rough split of 50 percent competency-based questions, 20 percent objective questions, and 30 percent short and long answers. There is no negative marking.

These percentages are approximate, so treat them as a guide for every subject. CBSE released the 2026 to 2027 sample question papers and marking schemes for Classes 10 and 12 in September 2026. They are on cbseacademic.nic.in and are the most reliable reference.

Students need at least 33 percent to pass. One educational source lists the minimum as 26 out of 80 in theory and 7 out of 20 in internal assessment. Other sources describe the rule differently, so confirm it in the latest official CBSE circular. CBSE awards grades on a nine-point scale.

Which Question Types Appear in the CBSE Class 10 Question Paper Pattern?

Papers combine multiple-choice questions, case-based questions, source-based questions, assertion-reasoning items, and short and long answers. Case and source questions test how well students apply a concept, not only how well they remember it.

A case-based question gives a short scenario, data table, or experiment, followed by linked questions. In Science, it may describe a laboratory experiment and ask about the reactions involved. A source-based question uses a passage, chart, or map extract. In Social Science, it may use a historical document excerpt and ask for interpretation. Assertion reasoning items ask whether a statement and its stated reason are both correct.

Competency questions reward regular practice with unfamiliar situations. How Should Students Prepare Using the Latest Paper Pattern?

Start with the official sample papers, then revise NCERT chapters, practise case-based questions every week, and attempt complete papers in three hours. Review every mistake against the official marking scheme.

  1. Download the 2026 to 2027 sample papers and marking schemes for every subject.
  2. Mark which units carry more marks in each subject's scheme.
  3. Revise NCERT text, examples, and exercises before moving to extra questions.
  4. Practise two or three case-based or source-based sets every week.
  5. Attempt one complete paper each week under exam conditions.
  6. Compare your answers with the marking scheme and note where steps were lost.
  7. Keep a mistake notebook and revisit it before each practice paper.

What Common Mistakes Should Students Avoid in the Board Paper?

Most lost marks come from ignoring application questions, skipping working in written answers, and relying on older paper patterns. Students should correct these habits early in the session.

Key Takeaways

  1. Each main subject carries 100 marks: 80 theory and 20 internal assessment.
  2. Roughly half the theory paper tests competency through case-based, source-based, source based and application questions.
  3. The 20 percent objective and 30 percent written answer split is a guide, so verify it in the official sample papers.
  4. Phase 1 runs from February 16 to March 9, 2027, and Phase 2 is optional for up to three subjects.

  6. Weekly timed practice with the marking scheme builds both speed and accuracy.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

FAQ 1: What is the CBSE Class 10 paper pattern for 2027?

Each main subject has an 80-mark theory paper and 20 marks of internal assessment. The theory paper mixes objective, competency-based, and written-answer questions. Check the official sample papers for each subject.

FAQ 2: How many questions are there in the Class 10 Mathematics paper?

The reported Mathematics pattern has 38 questions worth 80 marks. It includes 20 one-mark, 5 two-mark, 6 three-mark, 3 four-mark, and 4 five-mark questions.

FAQ 3: What is the competency-based question weightage in Class 10?

Competency-based questions carry about 50 percent of the theory marks, which is around 40 out of 80. They include case-based, source-based, source based and assertion-reasoning formats.

FAQ 4: Is there negative marking in the CBSE Class 10 board exam?

No. Educational sources reporting the 2027 pattern state there is no negative marking. Students should still avoid guessing blindly, because a considered answer scores better than a random one.

FAQ 5: What types of questions are asked in the Class 10 board paper?

Students face multiple-choice questions, case-based questions, source-based questions, assertion-reasoning items, and short and long answers. The mix varies by subject.

FAQ 6: Is the second board exam compulsory for Class 10 students?

No. Phase 1 is compulsory, and Phase 2 is optional. Students can use it to improve marks in up to three subjects, and it is expected in May 2027.
