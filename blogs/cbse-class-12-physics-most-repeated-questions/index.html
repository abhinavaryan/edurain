CBSE Class 12 Physics Most Repeated Questions in Board Exams (Chapter-Wise) | EduRain Blog

Board papers repeat core concepts, not identical sentences. Derivations, numericals on standard formulas, ray diagrams, and short reasoning questions return year after year. This guide lists those CBSE Class 12 Physics Most Repeated Questions unit by unit, with a preparation plan for the 2027 board exam.

How Board Paper Patterns Repeat Across Years

Examiners test the same core ideas in different formats. A derivation may appear as a 3-mark question one year and as a case-based question the next. Knowing this helps you study concepts rather than guess questions.

The theory paper has been 70 marks over 3 hours, with 1-mark, 2 mark, 3 mark, 4 mark case based and 5 mark sections. The practical carries 30 marks. Confirm the 2026 27 design and syllabus on the official CBSE Academic website, because patterns can change.

Everything below comes from past paper trends and the NCERT textbook, not from any official list. CBSE does not publish expected questions, and no one can guarantee what will appear.

Preparation Strategy for the 2027 Board Exam

Start with NCERT, then solve previous year papers by chapter, and finally attempt full timed papers. This order builds concept clarity first and speed later, which suits most students preparing for the 2027 boards.

  1. Read each NCERT chapter and mark every derivation and definition.
  2. Practise ray diagrams and circuit diagrams until you can draw them neatly in two minutes.
  3. Solve NCERT numericals, then sample papers released by CBSE.
  4. Analyse the last ten years of board papers and list repeated question types.
  5. Attempt two full papers a week in the last two months.

Structured resources can save time during this phase. The Edurain Online Education Platform can serve as one guided learning option when you want chapter-wise lectures and practice alongside your NCERT revision. Use any resource to support your own practice, not to replace it. After that, revise from your own marked notes.

Use a notebook to record errors from every test. Revisit that list weekly, because repeated mistakes cost more marks than unknown topics.

Write units with every numerical answer. Missing units often cost a mark even when the working is correct.

Common Mistakes That Lower Physics Scores

Most lost marks come from missing diagrams, wrong sign conventions, skipped units, and incomplete derivations. Careful presentation can improve a score without learning any new topic.

  1. Ray optics: Students forget arrows on rays or apply the sign convention inconsistently.
  2. Gauss's law: The Gaussian surface is not named or justified before applying the law.
  3. AC circuits: Phasor diagrams are drawn without labelling the phase angle.
  4. Numericals: Final answers appear without units or without the formula written first.
  5. Derivations: Intermediate steps are skipped, so step marks are lost.

Sample Practice Questions

Short practice sets reveal gaps faster than rereading. Try these before checking solutions in NCERT.

  1. Derive the electric field due to an infinite plane sheet of charge using Gauss's law.
  2. State Kirchhoff's rules and apply them to a Wheatstone bridge to derive the balance condition.
  3. Draw a ray diagram for a compound microscope and write the expression for its magnifying power.
  4. State Einstein's photoelectric equation and explain two features it accounts for.

Key Takeaways

  1. Board papers repeat concepts and question types, not exact wording.
  2. Derivations, ray diagrams and standard numericals form the most dependable revision core.
  3. Confirm the latest syllabus and paper design on the official CBSE Academic website.
  4. Previous year papers reveal patterns, but they never guarantee a future question.
  5. Neat diagrams, units, and step-by-step working protect marks you already know how to earn.
  6. Regular revision of the CBSE Class 12 Physics Most Repeated Questions in each chapter builds both speed and accuracy.

Conclusion and Next Steps

Repeated topics reward students who understand concepts and practise presentation. Pick one chapter today, solve its previous year questions, and note every mistake. Repeat this cycle chapter by chapter until the full syllabus is covered.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

FAQ 1: What are the most repeated questions in Class 12 Physics board exams?

Derivations such as Gauss's law applications, Biot-Savart law, lens maker's formula, and Young's double slit appear often. Numericals on circuits, capacitors, photoelectric effect, and nuclear decay also return regularly.

FAQ 2: Which questions are important for CBSE Class 12 Physics 2027?

Concept-based derivations, ray diagrams, case-based questions, and standard numericals are important. Check the official 2026 27 syllabus and sample paper, because CBSE can revise the pattern.

FAQ 3: What are the most important chapters in Class 12 Physics?

Electrostatics, current electricity, magnetic effects of current, electromagnetic induction, ray optics, and wave optics carry strong weight in many papers. Confirm the official marks distribution before deciding your study time.

FAQ 4: Which Physics questions are frequently asked in CBSE board exams?

Definitions, laws, short reasoning questions, and circuit or ray diagrams appear frequently. Long answer questions usually ask for derivations with a labelled diagram.

FAQ 5: How to prepare for the Class 12 Physics board exam 2027?

Finish NCERT first, practise derivations and numericals, then solve previous year and sample papers in timed conditions. Review your mistake notebook every week.

FAQ 6: Are previous year papers enough for Physics board preparation?

They show question patterns, but they are not enough alone. Combine them with NCERT examples, exercises, and sample papers so you can handle new framing of the same concepts.
