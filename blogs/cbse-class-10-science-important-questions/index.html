CBSE Class 10 Science Important Questions Chapter-Wise | EduRain Blog

The most reliable CBSE Class 10 Science Important Questions come from the NCERT textbook and exercise questions, followed by sample papers and case-based practice. Prepare chapter-wise, because Chemical Substances and the World of Living carry 25 marks each in the 80-mark theory paper.

Class 10 Science combines Chemistry, Biology, and Physics in one board paper. Many students do not know what to practise first. This guide covers the verified paper structure for 2027, unit marks, chapter-wise question types, key formulas, and common mistakes. Use it with Class 10 Science Important Questions 2027 practice from NCERT.

How Is the Class 10 Science Paper Structured for 2027?

The Science paper (subject code 086) carries 80 marks in theory and 20 marks for internal assessment. The theory paper runs for three hours. About 33 percent internal choice is provided.

CBSE tests three competencies in the following ratio.

CompetencyWeightage
Knowledge and understanding50%
Application of knowledge and concepts30%
Formulate, analyse, evaluate and create20%

Question types include very short answer and objective questions, assertion and reasoning, short answer, long answer, and case-based questions. Internal assessment has periodic assessment (5 plus 5 marks), practical work (5 marks), and a portfolio (5 marks).

CBSE released the Class 10 date sheet on 6 November 2026. Board exams run from 16 February to 9 March 2027 in pen and paper mode. Check the official date sheet for the Science paper date.

Which Units and Chapters Carry the Most Marks?

Chemical Substances and World of Living carry 25 marks each. Effects of Current carries 13 marks, Natural Phenomena 12 marks, and Natural Resources 5 marks. Together, they make an 80-mark theory paper. The chart below shows this split unit by unit.

The syllabus document also marks Periodic Classification of Elements, Heredity and Evolution, and the topics Motor, Electromagnetic Induction and Electric Generator as formative only. These are not assessed in the year-end examination. Confirm this against the latest CBSE syllabus PDF before planning revision.

How Should Students Practise Chapter-wise Questions Effectively?

Start with NCERT examples and exercises, attempt questions chapter-wise, then move to sample papers and case-based items. Review every wrong answer the same day and note the exact reason for the error.

  1. Read the NCERT chapter once and underline definitions, reactions, and diagrams.
  2. Solve all in-text and exercise questions without looking at answers.
  3. Attempt one set of Class 10 Science questions, chapter-wise, from NCERT Exemplar problems.
  4. Practise assertion-reasoning and case-based questions, since they test application.
  5. In the last month, revise one unit every week in a crash-course-style plan, then attempt full sample papers in three hours.

Regular feedback improves answer writing. Students who need guided practice can explore Best CBSE Class 10 Online Coaching options that include weekly Science tests and doubt solving. Confirm that any programme follows the latest CBSE syllabus and NCERT. Self-study with timed papers works well too.

Which Formulas Should Students Revise for Physics Questions?

Revise Ohm's law, resistance and power relations, and the mirror and lens formulas. Apply the sign convention and write units in every numerical answer, because marks are given for steps and units.

TopicFormula
Ohm's lawV = IR
ResistivityR = ρl/A
Series resistorsRs = R1 + R2 + R3
Parallel resistors1/Rp = 1/R1 + 1/R2 + 1/R3
Electric powerP = VI = I²R = V²/R
Heat producedH = I²Rt
Mirror formula and magnification1/v + 1/u = 1/f and m = −v/u
Lens formula and magnification1/v − 1/u = 1/f and m = v/u
Power of a lensP = 1/f, with f in metres

What Common Mistakes Cost Marks in Class 10 Science?

Most lost marks come from unbalanced equations, wrong sign convention, missing units, and unlabelled diagrams. Fixing these habits can improve scores faster than reading extra books.

  1. Write balanced equations with conditions, such as heat or a catalyst, where NCERT mentions them.
  2. Never skip the negative sign for u and f in mirror numericals.
  3. Label every diagram part and give the diagram a title.
  4. Read the passage in case-based questions twice before answering.
  5. Use scientific terms from NCERT instead of everyday words.

Key Takeaways

  1. The theory paper is 80 marks, plus 20 marks of internal assessment.
  2. Chemical Substances and World of Living together carry 50 of the 80 theory marks.
  3. CBSE Class 10 Science Important Questions work best when practised chapter-wise, starting from NCERT.
  4. Competency based, assertion reasoning and case-based questions need regular practice.
  5. Periodic Classification, Heredity and Evolution, and electromagnetic induction topics are formative only, as per the syllabus document.
  6. Numericals need given data, formula, substitution, answer, and unit.

Conclusion and Next Steps

Chapter-wise practice, accurate diagrams, and careful numericals build confidence for the board paper. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

FAQ 1: Which units carry the highest marks in Class 10 Science?

Chemical Substances: Nature and Behaviour and World of Living carry 25 marks each. Effects of Current carries 13 marks, Natural Phenomena 12 marks, and Natural Resources 5 marks in the theory paper.

FAQ 2: Are NCERT questions enough for the board exam?

NCERT is the prescribed textbook, so it is the base. Add NCERT Exemplar problems and CBSE sample papers to practise application and case-based questions.

FAQ 3: What types of questions appear in the Science paper?

Expect objective and very short answer questions, assertion and reasoning, short answer, long answer, and case-based questions. About 33 percent internal choice is provided.

FAQ 4: Which Physics formulas are most important?

Learn V = IR, R = ρl/A, the series and parallel resistor formulas, P = VI, the mirror and lens formulas, and the power of a lens.

FAQ 5: How can students practise numerical questions better?

Write the given data, the formula, the substitution, and the final answer with units. Apply the sign convention in light chapters and check each calculation once.

FAQ 6: How does the two-exam system work for Class 10?

Every student appears in the first phase. Students who wish to improve their marks can take the second phase in up to three subjects.
