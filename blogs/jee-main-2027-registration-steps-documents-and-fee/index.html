JEE Main 2027 Registration: Step-by-Step Application Process, Fees, and Documents | EduRain Blog

Candidates complete JEE Main 2027 Registration online on the NTA portal, jeemain.nta.nic.in. They register, fill the form, upload a photograph, signature, and documents, pay the fee, and save the confirmation page. NTA has not yet announced the official application dates. This guide separates confirmed details from expectations.

When Will the JEE Main 2027 Application Form Open?

NTA has not announced the application dates. Reports expect the Session 1 form around the fourth week of October 2026, based on earlier cycles. Treat this as an expectation until NTA publishes its notice and information bulletin.

Reports point to the fourth week of October 2026 for Session 1 and the first week of February 2027 for Session 2. Once the window opens, JEE Main 2027 Registration will run only on the official portal, jeemain.nta.nic.in.

For the 2026 cycle, NTA's notice inviting Session 1 applications was uploaded to its website at the end of October 2025. That is a past pattern, not a promise for 2027.

When this guide was prepared, the NTA homepage did not yet show a 2027 notice. Always confirm dates on the official site.

What Is the Step-by-Step Application Process?

The process is online. Create a new account, fill in personal and academic details, upload images and documents, pay the fee, and download the confirmation page. NTA will confirm any 2027 changes in its bulletin.

Follow this order once the link goes live:

  1. Open jeemain.nta.nic.in and click the application link for JEE Main 2027.
  2. Create a new account using your own mobile number and email address.
  3. Log in with the credentials you receive.
  4. Fill in personal details, academic details, and your exam city choices.
  5. Select the paper or papers you will attempt. Paper 1 is for B.E. or B.Tech. Papers 2A and 2B cover B.Arch and B.Planning.
  6. Upload your photograph, signature, and any required certificates in the prescribed format.
  7. Pay the fee online, then preview and submit the form.
  8. Download the confirmation page and keep a copy safely.

Multiple applications can lead to cancellation or other action under NTA rules, so submit one clean form only.

Which Documents Should You Keep Ready?

NTA lists the final documents in its information bulletin. Based on recent cycles, keep an identity proof, a recent photograph, a signature, academic details, category or disability certificates if applicable, and payment details. Confirm the list after the 2027 bulletin.

Current guidance expects academic records, identity details, a recent photograph, a signature, and valid category or disability certificates where applicable. Treat this as a preparation checklist, not the official list.

What Is the Application Fee for JEE Main 2027?

NTA has not announced the 2027 fee. The amount will come from the final bulletin. It has usually varied by category, number of papers, and exam centre location. Do not rely on unofficial figures before the notification.

The fee is revised in each year's bulletin, so last year's amounts may not apply. Pay only through the official payment page shown after you submit your details. Check that your payment status shows as successful before you leave the portal.

Fees were reported as not refundable, so confirm the exact policy in the 2027 bulletin before paying.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

FAQ 1: Has the application window for JEE Main 2027 opened?

Not as of mid-October 2026 reports. NTA has not published the official dates. Check jeemain.nta.nic.in for the notice and information bulletin.

FAQ 2: Can I fill the JEE Main 2027 form offline?

No. The application is completed through the online portal. Save the confirmation page after submission.

FAQ 3: Is Aadhaar mandatory for the 2027 application?

NTA has not confirmed the 2027 identity rules. Follow the information bulletin and use only the identity proof types it allows.

FAQ 4: Can I change details after submitting the form?

NTA has opened correction windows in recent cycles. The 2027 correction dates are not announced, so enter every detail carefully the first time.

FAQ 5: How many sessions will JEE Main 2027 have?

Two sessions are expected. Session 1 is reported for January 2027. Session 2 is expected around April 2027, but NTA has not confirmed it.

FAQ 6: What should I prepare before the form opens?

Keep your identity proof, scanned photograph and signature, academic details, applicable certificates, and payment method ready. Confirm the final list in the bulletin.
