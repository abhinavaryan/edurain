JEE Main 2027 Physics Formula Sheet: Chapter-Wise Important Formulas | EduRain Blog

A JEE Main 2027 Physics Formula Sheet gives you the core equations from mechanics, heat, electricity, optics, and modern physics in one place. Use it for quick revision after you learn each concept from NCERT and class notes. Always check the topic list against the official NTA syllabus for 2027.

Mechanics and Properties of Matter Formulas

Mechanics covers motion, force, energy, rotation, gravitation, oscillations, and fluids. These equations support many later chapters, so learn them first and always note the units and conditions beside each one.

TopicKey Formulas
Kinematicsv = u + at; s = ut + ½at²; v² = u² + 2as
Projectile motionRange R = u² sin2θ / g; Height H = u² sin²θ / 2g; Time T = 2u sinθ / g
Laws of motionF = ma; friction f ≤ μN
Work, energy, powerW = Fs cosθ; KE = ½mv²; P = F·v
Circular motiona = v²/r; F = mv²/r
Rotationτ = Iα; L = Iω; KE = ½Iω²
GravitationF = Gm₁m₂/r²; escape velocity = √(2GM/R)
SHMT = 2π√(m/k); ω = 2π/T
Elasticity and fluidsY = stress/strain; P + ½ρv² + ρgh = constant; Stokes force = 6πηrv

Write the vector form wherever a formula involves direction. Many wrong answers come from ignoring signs in kinematics and rotation. These are the JEE Main 2027 Physics important formulas you will use most often in numerical problems.

Heat, Waves, Electricity and Magnetism Formulas

These chapters depend on standard conditions and sign conventions. Learn each equation along with the situation where it applies, because the same symbol can mean different things in different chapters.

TopicKey Formulas
CalorimetryQ = mcΔT; Q = mL
Ideal gasPV = nRT; average KE per molecule = (3/2)kT
ThermodynamicsΔU = Q − W; Carnot efficiency = 1 − T₂/T₁
Wavesv = fλ; y = A sin(kx − ωt)
Closed pipeFundamental frequency f = v/4L
ElectrostaticsF = kq₁q₂/r²; E = kq/r²; V = kq/r; U = ½CV²
CapacitorC = ε₀A/d
Current electricityV = IR; R = ρl/A; P = I²R
Magnetic forceF = qvB sinθ; F = BIl sinθ
Magnetic fieldInfinite wire B = μ₀I/2πr; solenoid B = μ₀nI
Inductionemf = −dΦ/dt; motional emf = Blv
Alternating currentZ = √(R² + (X_L − X_C)²); resonance ω = 1/√(LC)

The magnetic field at the centre of a circular loop is μ₀I/2R. Keep it separate from the straight wire formula. Average power in an AC circuit is VI cosφ, so the phase angle matters.

Plan to Revise Physics Formulas

Revise formulas in a fixed cycle: learn the concept, derive the formula once, solve questions, then review weekly. This builds recall that lasts under exam pressure and reduces careless errors.

  1. Finish one chapter from NCERT before touching its formulas.
  2. Write every formula on a single page with units and conditions.
  3. Solve ten to fifteen numerical questions using only that page.
  4. Mark the formulas you forgot and revisit them after two days.
  5. Attempt a timed chapter test every week.

Optics, Modern Physics and Semiconductor Formulas

Optics and modern physics have fewer formulas, but each appears often in numerical questions. Pay close attention to sign conventions, because a wrong sign changes the image distance or energy value completely.

Lens formula1/v − 1/u = 1/f
Lens maker1/f = (n − 1)(1/R₁ − 1/R₂)
Refractionn₁ sinθ₁ = n₂ sinθ₂
Young's double slitFringe width β = λD/d
Photoelectric effectE = hν; K max = hν − φ
de Broglie wavelengthλ = h/p
Bohr model (hydrogen)Eₙ = −13.6/n² eV
RadioactivityN = N₀e^(−λt); half life = 0.693/λ

For semiconductors, concentrate on logic gates, diode behaviour, and the working of a junction diode. These topics are mostly conceptual.

Common Mistakes While Using Formulas

Most lost marks come from applying the right formula in the wrong situation. Check units, signs, and conditions before you substitute any values.

  1. Mixing units, such as centimetres with metres.
  2. Using the lens formula with mirror sign conventions.
  3. Applying constant acceleration equations to variable acceleration problems.
  4. Forgetting that AC power depends on the phase angle.
  5. Memorising formulas without understanding what each symbol means.

Fix these by writing the given data with units before substitution. Then verify your final answer with a rough estimate.

Practice Questions with Solutions

Solve these questions without looking at your formula page, then compare your working with the solutions. This tests whether you can recall and apply formulas under pressure, not just recognise them.

Question 1: A car starts from rest with acceleration 2 m/s². Find the distance covered in 5 seconds.

Using s = ut + ½at², s = 0 + ½ × 2 × 25 = 25 m.

Question 2: A 10 Ω resistor is connected to a 5 V source. Find the current and power.

I = V/R = 0.5 A. P = VI = 2.5 W.

Question 3: A convex lens of focal length 20 cm forms an image of an object placed 30 cm away. Find the image distance.

With u = −30 cm, 1/v = 1/20 − 1/30 = 1/60. So v = 60 cm.

Key Takeaways

  1. Use your JEE Main 2027 Physics Formula Sheet only after you understand each concept.
  2. Always note conditions, units, and sign conventions beside every formula.
  3. Mechanics, electricity and magnetism, and optics need frequent revision.
  4. Solve numerical questions on the same day you revise a formula.
  5. Confirm the final syllabus on the official NTA website.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

FAQ 1: Which chapters have the most formulas in JEE Main Physics?

Mechanics, electrostatics, current electricity, magnetism, optics, and modern physics need the most formula practice. Thermodynamics and waves also contain several equations that appear regularly in numerical problems.

FAQ 2: Should I memorise formulas or derive them?

Do both. Derive each formula at least once to understand its conditions, then memorise the final form. This helps you avoid using it in the wrong situation.

FAQ 3: How often should I revise the formula page?

Revise it briefly every day and in full once a week. Spaced revision improves long-term recall better than a single long session.

FAQ 4: Are constants and values given in the JEE Main paper?

Do not rely on this. Learn common constants such as g, G, h, and e, and check the instructions in recent NTA papers to see what is provided.

FAQ 5: Can I prepare Physics with only a formula page?

No. A formula page is a revision tool. You also need NCERT reading, concept clarity, and regular numerical practice.

FAQ 6: Which formulas do students confuse most often in JEE Main Physics?

Students often mix the lens and mirror formulas, and the magnetic field of a straight wire with that of a circular loop. Capacitors in series and parallel are also commonly reversed. Write these pairs side by side with their conditions and revise them together.
