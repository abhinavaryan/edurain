Class 10 Maths Important Questions with Answers for CBSE Board Exam 2027 | EduRain Blog

The most important questions in Class 10 Maths come from Algebra, Geometry, Trigonometry, Mensuration, and Statistics, because these units carry most of the 80 theory marks. Practising NCERT examples, exercise problems, and case study questions with correct steps is the most reliable way to prepare for the 2027 board exam.

This guide brings together Class 10 Maths Important Questions with Answers, unit-wise marks, key formulas, solved problems, and a practical preparation plan for the 2026 to 2027 session. The questions follow the Mathematics Standard paper. Students of Mathematics Basic can use the same concepts at a simpler level.

Which Chapters Carry the Most Marks in Class 10 Maths?

Algebra carries the highest weightage, with 20 marks out of 80 in the theory paper. Geometry, Trigonometry, Statistics and Probability, and Mensuration follow. The paper also has 20 marks for internal assessment.

How Can Students Prepare for the Class 10 Maths Board Exam 2027?

Start with the NCERT textbook, solve every example and exercise, then move to sample papers and previous year questions. Revise formulas daily and practise timed papers so that accuracy and speed improve together.

Follow these steps in order:

  1. Finish the NCERT examples first, then the exercises, chapter by chapter.
  2. Make a one-page formula sheet for each unit and revise it every morning.
  3. Practise NCERT-based important questions, then move to the CBSE question bank and sample papers.
  4. Attempt one full paper every week in three hours, without checking notes.
  5. Keep an error notebook and revisit every mistake after seven days.

Some students also want regular doubt solving and weekly tests. In that case, joining the Best CBSE Class 10 Online Coaching can help, provided the classes follow NCERT closely and give feedback on your answers. Even then, self-practice remains the main driver of marks, so keep solving questions on your own.

Which Solved Questions Should Every Student Practise?

Solved questions teach the exact steps examiners reward. The set of Class 10 Maths Important Questions with Answers below covers proofs, equations, progressions, trigonometry, heights and distances, mensuration and probability. Use them as a model for presenting answers in the board exam.

Question 1 (Real Numbers). Prove that √3 is irrational.

Answer. Assume √3 = p/q, where p and q are coprime integers, and q is not zero. Squaring gives p² = 3q², so 3 divides p², which means 3 divides p. Write p = 3m. Then 9m² = 3q², so q² = 3m², and 3 divides q. Now 3 divides both p and q, which contradicts our assumption. Hence √3 is irrational.

Question 2 (Quadratic Equations). Solve 2x² − 7x + 3 = 0.

Answer. Split the middle term: 2x² − 6x − x + 3 = 0. This gives 2x(x − 3) − 1(x − 3) = 0, so (2x − 1)(x − 3) = 0. Therefore x = 1/2 or x = 3.

Question 3 (Arithmetic Progressions). Find the sum of the first 20 terms of the AP 5, 8, 11, and so on.

Answer. Here a = 5, d = 3 and n = 20. S₂₀ = 20/2 [2(5) + 19(3)] = 10 × 67 = 670.

Question 4 (Trigonometry). Prove that sin θ/(1 + cos θ) + (1 + cos θ)/sin θ = 2 cosec θ.

Answer. Take the left side over a common denominator: [sin²θ + (1 + cos θ)²] / [sin θ (1 + cos θ)]. The numerator becomes sin²θ + 1 + 2cos θ + cos²θ = 2 + 2cos θ = 2(1 + cos θ). Cancelling (1 + cos θ) leaves 2/sin θ, which is 2 cosec θ.

Question 5 (Heights and Distances). From a point 30 m away from the foot of a tower, the angle of elevation of the top is 60°. Find the height of the tower.

Answer. tan 60° = height/30, so height = 30√3 m. Using √3 = 1.732, the height is about 51.96 m.

Question 6 (Surface Areas and Volumes). A cone has radius 7 cm and height 24 cm. Find its curved surface area and volume.

Answer. Slant height l = √(7² + 24²) = √625 = 25 cm. CSA = πrl = (22/7) × 7 × 25 = 550 cm². Volume = ⅓πr²h = ⅓ × (22/7) × 49 × 24 = 1232 cm³.

Question 7 (Probability). Two dice are thrown together. Find the probability that the sum is 8.

Answer. Favourable outcomes are (2,6), (3,5), (4,4), (5,3) and (6,2), which makes 5. Total outcomes are 36. So P = 5/36.

What Practice Questions Help in Last Minute Revision?

Short numerical questions from each unit are ideal for the final weeks. Solve these without looking at the answers, then check your steps carefully against the key given below.

  1. Find the HCF and LCM of 96 and 404 using prime factorisation.
  2. Find the values of k for which 2x² + kx + 3 = 0 has equal roots.
  3. Find the distance between the points (−5, 7) and (−1, 3).
  4. For a modal class 20 to 30 with f₁ = 12, f₀ = 5, f₂ = 7 and h = 10, find the mode.
  5. A point is 13 cm from the centre of a circle of radius 5 cm. Find the length of the tangent from the point.

Answer key: 1) HCF = 4, LCM = 9696. 2) k = ±2√6, since k² − 24 = 0. 3) 4√2 units. 4) Mode = 20 + (7/12) × 10 ≈ 25.83. 5) √(169 − 25) = 12 cm.

Which Common Mistakes Cost Marks in Maths Board Exams?

Most lost marks come from skipped steps, wrong signs, missing units, and weak diagrams. Careful presentation fixes these errors more quickly than learning new topics, so build habits early.

  1. Skipping steps: Write each line of working. CBSE awards marks for method, not only the final answer.
  2. Sign errors in quadratics: Check the signs of a, b, and c before using the formula.
  3. Missing units: Always write cm, cm² or cm³ in mensuration answers.
  4. Wrong angle values: Learn the standard trigonometric ratio table for 0°, 30°, 45°, 60° and 90°.
  5. Untidy diagrams: Label every point and angle in geometry and heights and distances questions.
  6. Rounding too early: Keep exact values such as √3 until the last step.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

FAQ 1: What are the most important questions in Class 10 Maths for 2027?

Proofs on irrational numbers, quadratic equations, arithmetic progressions, trigonometric identities, heights and distances, mensuration, and probability are regularly practised. Treat them as most expected questions based on past patterns, not as official predictions.

FAQ 2: Which chapters are important in Class 10 Maths CBSE?

Algebra has the highest weightage at 20 marks. Geometry, Trigonometry, Statistics and Probability, and Mensuration also carry significant marks. Still, all 14 NCERT chapters should be covered.

FAQ 3: How to prepare for CBSE Class 10 Maths Board Exam 2027?

Complete NCERT examples and exercises first. Then solve the question bank, sample papers, and previous years' papers. Revise formulas daily and attempt one timed paper every week.

FAQ 4: What are the most important formulas for Class 10 Maths?

Remember the quadratic formula, AP formulas, distance and section formulas, trigonometric identities, cone and sphere formulas, and the mode and median formulas. The formula table in this guide lists them together.

FAQ 5: Which topics are most scoring in Class 10 Maths?

Arithmetic progressions, coordinate geometry, trigonometric ratios, mensuration, and probability are often scoring because their steps are clear. Marks depend on accurate practice, so no topic should be skipped.

FAQ 6: How to solve difficult Maths questions in Class 10?

Read the question twice, draw a diagram where possible, and list the given data. Choose the relevant formula, solve step by step, and recheck the signs and units before writing the final answer.
