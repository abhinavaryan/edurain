NEET UG 2027 Registration Date, Eligibility & Guide

Every NEET aspirant needs two answers first: am I eligible, and when can I apply? This guide covers age limits, qualifying marks, subject rules, documents, and the expected application timeline. It also explains how to plan your preparation while the official notice is pending. All dates below come from the earlier NTA cycle until NTA confirms the new schedule.

What Are the Eligibility Criteria for NEET UG 2027?

Eligibility for NEET UG 2027 depends on age, Class 12 subjects, qualifying marks, and nationality. NTA sets these rules in its Information Bulletin, so verify each point there once it is released.

CriterionRequirement (based on the earlier NTA cycle)
Minimum age17 years on or before 31 December of the admission year
Upper age limitNone at present, subject to the latest notification
SubjectsPhysics, Chemistry, Biology or Biotechnology, and English
Qualifying marks in PCBGeneral 50%, OBC, SC, ST 40%, General PwBD 45%
Number of attemptsNo fixed limit
NationalityIndian citizens, NRIs, OCI, PIO, and foreign nationals

Age Limit and Number of Attempts

You must turn 17 by 31 December of the year you seek admission. There is no upper age limit under the current policy. You can also attempt NEET as many times as you wish. Always check the bulletin, because the NTA can revise these rules.

Academic Qualification and Qualifying Marks

You need Class 12 or an equivalent exam with Physics, Chemistry, Biology or Biotechnology, and English. The marks are counted in Physics, Chemistry, and Biology together.

Students appearing in Class 12 in 2027 can apply. Their admission is subject to passing with the required marks. Open school and private candidates are also eligible if they meet the subject rules.

Category and Nationality Rules

Reserved category candidates must hold valid certificates in the format NTA prescribes. Check the issue date rules for OBC (NCL) and EWS certificates carefully. Foreign nationals, NRIs, OCI, and PIO candidates may need extra documents. The bulletin lists them clearly.

NTA has not announced the NEET UG 2027 registration date yet. Based on the earlier cycle, applications are expected to open in February 2027 on the official NTA NEET website.

Documents Needed Before You Apply

Keep your Class 10 certificate, photograph, signature, identity proof, and category certificate ready before the window opens. Early preparation avoids upload errors and last-minute fixes during peak traffic. Specifications for each file appear in the bulletin.

Follow these steps once registration opens:

  1. Visit neet.nta.nic.in and open the new registration link.
  2. Create your login with your name, mobile number, and email.
  3. Fill in personal, academic, and exam city details.
  4. Upload your photograph, signature, and required certificates.
  5. Pay the application fee online.
  6. Download and save the confirmation page.

The fee may change from year to year. Confirm the amount in the 2027 bulletin.

How to Use the Waiting Time for NEET 2027 Online Preparation

Use the weeks before registration to follow a fixed daily routine built on NCERT. Structured NEET 2027 Online Preparation works best with timed tests, chapter revision, and error tracking. Biology carries about twice the marks of Physics or Chemistry, so it deserves daily time.

SubjectMain focus
PhysicsFormula practice, unit analysis, and solved numericals by topic
ChemistryPhysical numericals, organic reaction maps, inorganic NCERT facts
BiologyNCERT lines, diagrams, tables, and exceptions in Botany and Zoology


No syllabus change has been announced for 2027 so far. Follow the syllabus on the official NTA and NMC pages and avoid deleted portions.

A simple routine looks like this:

  1. Read NCERT Biology line by line, then solve chapter questions.
  2. Revise Chemistry in three parts: physical, organic, and inorganic.
  3. Practice Physics numericals by topic, then move to mixed sets.
  4. Take one timed mock test every week as part of your NEET 2027 Online Preparation.
  5. Record every mistake in an error notebook and revisit it on Sundays.

Common Mistakes Students Make

Most registration problems come from small errors, not hard rules. Avoid these:

  1. Treating an unofficial website date as the confirmed schedule.
  2. Ignoring the PCB qualifying marks rule before applying.
  3. Entering a date of birth that does not match the Class 10 certificate.
  4. Using different spellings of your name across documents.
  5. Uploading a photograph or signature in the wrong size or format.
  6. Filling the form on a lookalike portal instead of the official site.
  7. Missing the correction window, which usually stays open only a few days.

Some fields, such as date of birth, are generally not editable later. Check every entry before final submission.

Key Takeaways

  1. The minimum age is 17 years by 31 December of the admission year.
  2. Physics, Chemistry, Biology or Biotechnology, and English are compulsory in Class 12.
  3. Track the NEET UG 2027 registration date only on the official NTA website.
  4. Registration is expected in February 2027, but this is not yet confirmed.
  5. Keep documents and photographs ready before the window opens.
  6. Build a daily study routine around NCERT, weekly mocks, and an error notebook.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is the minimum age to apply for NEET UG 2027?

You must be at least 17 years old on or before 31 December of the admission year. Confirm the exact rule in the official bulletin.

2. Is there an upper age limit for NEET UG 2027?

There is no upper age limit under the current policy. NTA can change this, so check the new notification when it is released.

3. How many marks do I need in Class 12 to be eligible?

General category students need 50% in Physics, Chemistry, and Biology together. OBC, SC, and ST candidates need 40%. General PwBD candidates need 45%.

4. Can Class 12 appearing students register for NEET UG 2027?

Yes, students appearing in Class 12 in 2027 can apply. They must still pass with the required subjects and marks to be admitted.

5. When will applications open for NEET UG 2027?

NTA has not announced dates yet. Applications are expected to open in February 2027, based on the earlier cycle.

6. Can I correct mistakes after submitting the application?

NTA usually opens a short correction window after registration closes. Some fields, like date of birth, are generally not editable, so enter them carefully.
