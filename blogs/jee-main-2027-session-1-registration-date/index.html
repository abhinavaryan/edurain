JEE Main 2027 Session 1 Registration Date | EduRain Blog

Every JEE aspirant wants one clear answer in October: when does the application form open? NTA has already published the Session 1 exam dates for January 2027. The official JEE Mains 2027 Session 1 Registration Date is not yet announced. Students must rely on the usual NTA pattern until the information bulletin arrives. This guide explains the expected opening and closing window, the confirmed exam schedule, and the documents to keep ready. It also gives a step-by-step plan to apply without errors, the mistakes to avoid, and answers to six common questions.

Latest Status of Session 1 Registration

As of October 1, 2026, NTA has not officially confirmed the Session 1 application dates. Current reports expect the process to begin around October 2026, but this is an expected timeline, not a confirmed NTA date.

Treat every date except the exam dates as tentative. Check only official sources: jeemain.nta.nic.in and nta.ac.in. NTA usually releases the public notice and the information bulletin together.

Expected Registration Timeline

The JEE Main 2027 Session 1 Registration Date is expected in the fourth week of October 2026. The last date to apply is expected in the fourth week of November 2026. The window usually stays open for 27 to 28 days.

Confirmed Session 1 Exam Dates

NTA's calendar places Session 1 on January 22, 23, 24, 28, 29, and 30, 2027. January 31 is kept as a buffer date, and the test is computer-based.

Your registration must be completed well before the city intimation slip in January. Session 2 has its own registration and fee payment. It opens only after the January session.

Documents to Keep Ready Before the Form Opens

Keep a scanned photograph, signature, identity proof, and Class 10 certificate ready before registration opens. Preparing early avoids rejection caused by wrong file size or format.

  1. A recent photograph, as per bulletin specifications
  2. A scanned signature
  3. Class 10 certificate or marksheet for name and date of birth
  4. A valid identity proof
  5. Category or PwD certificate, if applicable
  6. An active mobile number and email ID
  7. An online payment method for the application fee

Exact file size and format limits will be listed in the bulletin. Scan your documents again once those limits are published.

Step-by-Step Plan to Complete Registration

Registration is a short online process, but one wrong entry can create problems later. Follow these steps in order once the form opens.

  1. Open jeemain.nta.nic.in and read the information bulletin fully.
  2. Create your account using your own mobile number and email ID.
  3. Fill in personal details exactly as printed on your Class 10 certificate.
  4. Select your paper: BE or BTech, BArch, or BPlanning.
  5. Choose your exam cities in the order of preference.
  6. Upload the photograph, signature, and other documents in the required format.
  7. Pay the fee online. The amount depends on category and paper, so check the bulletin.
  8. Download the confirmation page and save a copy.

Using the Waiting Period Wisely

Use the weeks before the form opens to organise documents and fix a revision schedule. Registration itself needs little time, so most of your effort should go into preparation.

Students preparing through online classes for JEE Main 2027 should ask their faculty for a study plan that concludes before January 22. You can also join online classes for JEE Main 2027 for structured guidance and exam-focused preparation.

Common Mistakes Students Make During Registration

Most registration problems come from rushed entries and unofficial sources. These are the errors to avoid.

  1. Waiting for the last day. Server load rises near the deadline, and payment can fail.
  2. Using unofficial websites. Apply only through the NTA portals.
  3. Mismatched details. Name or date of birth must match the Class 10 certificate.
  4. Wrong photo or signature size. Uploads get rejected if they miss the limits.
  5. Wrong category selection. Choose it carefully and keep the certificate ready.
  6. Using someone else's phone or email. You may miss important messages from NTA.
  7. Ignoring the correction window. Dates for correction are announced separately.

Key Takeaways

  1. NTA has not confirmed the Session 1 application dates yet.
  2. The form is expected in the fourth week of October 2026.
  3. The last date is expected in the fourth week of November 2026.
  4. Session 1 exam dates are January 22, 23, 24, 28, 29, and 30, 2027.
  5. Keep your photograph, signature, Class 10 certificate, and identity proof ready.
  6. Follow only jeemain.nta.nic.in and nta.ac.in for official updates.

Conclusion

Registration is simple when your documents are ready and your details are accurate. Start today by scanning every document and checking each detail against your Class 10 certificate. Visit the NTA website every few days for the bulletin. Frequently Asked Questions

1. When will the Session 1 application form open?

It is expected in the fourth week of October 2026. NTA has not confirmed this yet. Check jeemain.nta.nic.in for the official notice.

2. What is the expected last date to apply for Session 1?

The last date is expected in the fourth week of November 2026. The window usually stays open for about 27 to 28 days. Wait for the bulletin to confirm.

3. Where should I register for Session 1?

Register only on the official NTA portal, jeemain.nta.nic.in. Avoid third-party links, as they may collect your data or charge extra fees.

4. What are the Session 1 exam dates?

Session 1 will be held on January 22, 23, 24, 28, 29, and 30, 2027. January 31, 2027, is the buffer date.

5. Which documents are needed for registration?

You need a photograph, signature, Class 10 certificate, identity proof, and a category certificate if applicable. Also keep an active mobile number and email ID.

6. Can I apply for both Session 1 and Session 2?

Yes. You can appear in both sessions. Each session has a separate registration and fee payment, so complete the process again for Session 2.
