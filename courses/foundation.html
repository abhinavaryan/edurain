Why choose EduRain online classes for Class 6 to 12?

  • Live online classes. Attend live classes from home and ask questions the moment they come to mind. Teachers explain, solve examples on the board and keep the class active.
  • Recorded lectures for revision. Missed a class or want to revisit a chapter before the exam? Watch the recording again as many times as you need.
  • DPPs for every chapter. Daily Practice Problems make practice a daily habit instead of a last-week rush.
  • Weekly tests and mock tests. Weekly tests show where you stand, and mock tests prepare you for the real exam hall. After every test, review your weak topics and fix them.
  • Doubt-clearing support. Ask doubts in class or through our doubt support and get simple, clear explanations from teachers.
  • Board exam preparation. From Class 9 onwards, and especially in Class 10 and 12, we keep the board pattern in mind: NCERT-based teaching, important questions, previous-year question practice, revision and answer-writing.
  • Free demo class. Attend a free demo class, see how we teach and then decide.
  • App and web access. Study on the EduRain app or website, on a phone, tablet or laptop, whenever it suits you.

Subjects covered in Class 6 to 12 online coaching

  • Maths: number systems, algebra, geometry, mensuration, trigonometry and statistics in Class 6 to 10, and sets, functions, calculus, vectors, probability and more in Class 11 and 12, taught with solved examples and practice sets.
  • Science: physics, chemistry and biology explained with diagrams, real-life examples and numerical practice. In Class 11 and 12, Physics, Chemistry and Biology are taught as separate subjects.
  • English: grammar, writing skills and reading comprehension for better school and board exam answers.
  • Social Science (SST): history, geography, civics and economics for Class 6 to 10, with chapter-wise notes and answer-writing practice, taught by Khushboo ma'am.

How a typical week looks

  1. Attend live classes on the chapter of the week.
  2. Revise the same day with the recorded lecture and notes.
  3. Solve DPPs on that chapter.
  4. Clear doubts with teachers.
  5. Take the weekly test and review your mistakes.

This simple cycle of learn, practise, test and improve is what turns average marks into good marks.

From foundation to JEE and NEET: why start early?

Many toppers say their rank was built in Class 9 and 10. Chapters like motion, force, atoms and molecules, life processes, electricity and algebra are the roots of Class 11 and 12 Physics, Chemistry, Biology and Maths. When these basics are clear early, serious exam preparation in Class 11 and 12 becomes smoother and less stressful. And once your child reaches Class 11 and 12, the same EduRain classroom carries them through school exams, boards and entrance tests together.
