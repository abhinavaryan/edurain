JEE Main 2027 Registration: Eligibility, Documents, and Application Process

JEE Main 2027 Session 1 registration began on 24 October 2026 on the official NTA website, and the last date to apply is 22 November 2026 (11:50 pm). Candidates who want admission to B.E./B.Tech, B.Arch and B.Planning courses at NITs, IIITs and other participating institutes can apply online.

Before you fill the application form, check the JEE Main 2027 eligibility criteria, the documents required, the application fee and the registration steps. You will typically need a recent photograph, your signature, Class 10 details and a valid ID, but always confirm the exact list in the official NTA notice. Keep your documents ready and check the latest updates on the application form, exam schedule, syllabus and other notifications regularly, so that you never miss a deadline.

How To Plan Your Next Step from JEE Main to IIT Admission?

For many students, JEE Main is the gateway to JEE Advanced and, finally, the IITs. If your percentile meets the required cutoff, you become eligible to appear for JEE Advanced 2027.

At this stage your strategy needs a small shift. JEE Main focuses on speed, accuracy and broad syllabus coverage, while JEE Advanced tests deeper concept clarity, multi-concept application and advanced problem-solving.

EduRain supports this transition by:
